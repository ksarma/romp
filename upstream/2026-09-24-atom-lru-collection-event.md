Upstream ships the same LRU (weak ownership, no weakref callback, dead entries left for the cap or an id-reuse pop), so the growth and the fix apply as they are. fix tier: on the unfixed event model 13 tests in tests/test_asm_mat_lru_release.py are red because a freed list's entries are still held, each on a (live, dead) entry count such as (1, 5) != (1, 0); 10 more fail there on names the fix adds and 1 on the length hardening, and the module's other 5 tests, unchanged from main, pass; the residual witness (FinalizerRead), which runs on CPython 3.13 from 3.13.13 and on 3.14.4 and later and skips where the interpreter runs the callback of a weak reference a finalizer creates to an object the same collection frees, fails there too, its finalizer stopped by the missing _ref (an AttributeError the interpreter reports and ignores); the asmIndex key-set pin in tests/test_perf_stats.py fails there on the missing collected key. The cap is unchanged; it stays a backstop above the live entries.
