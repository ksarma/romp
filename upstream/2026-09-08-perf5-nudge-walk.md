An UPSTREAM REGRESSION fix. Upstream commits 4a3160e3 and 6275443d (PR #936, 2026-09-05; folded into the fork by the upmerge #349) made `_auto_nudge_tick` run the per-session walk in wake-only mode when auto-nudge is off, so the awaiting dead-man for kind=job stamps is reachable. Before them the tick returned at once with the toggle off; after them the walk paid, for every alive session on every pusher cycle, a writer-style `jd.load_goals` (a parse plus the journal replay) and the planner-placement gate's `jd.plan_units` and `jd._segs` over every turn, before the wake-only branch decided anything. On the live kernel (37 sessions, 0.65 cycles/s, toggle off) that was 2540-2568 of about 8400 GIL samples in two 120 s profiles: 30% of all samples, 21% of a core. The fix keeps the walk's control flow, filings and journal rows exactly as they are: the decision reads the store through `jd.load_goals_shared` (every row the walk files is already written on a fresh writer copy at the filing moment; `_file_wake_answer`, the one writer that took the walk's store, now loads its own); the placement gate is memoized per session on the identity of the parsed turns, the identity of the shared FrozenStore, and the two files the computation reads beyond them, the sid's episodes log (`_placed_key` -> `episode_floor`) and `cleared.jsonl` (`plan_units` -> `_live_anchor_gone` -> `_view_cleared`), which is the plan tier's `_sig_inputs` list minus the planner-only reads; `_all_outstanding_delegated` rides the same entry on the store object. Measured on the sealed state copy (31 alive sessions, 21 reaching the gate, closer settled as the live judges keep it): `_auto_nudge_tick` 253.5 ms CPU per warm cycle before, 20.1 ms after (plan_units 21 to 0 calls, _segs 3490 to 0, writer loads 21 to 0, 21 shared reads, 21 memo hits); with the closer gate holding, 42.5 to 19.4 ms. perf-bench `push_steady` unchanged within noise (105.4 to 102.8 ms): its `_push` never ran the tick. A reorder that evaluated the dead-man predicate first was rejected because the closer and placement gates decide whether the goal loop runs that cycle and the loop's per-goal walk-gate rows are read by `_awaiting_wake_outcomes` with the toggle off.

Resolved upstream 2026-09-09: their #1141 (b544ee8e) reads the auto-nudge walk through load_goals_shared_or_fault and memoizes _nudge_placement_gate per sid on the parse, the same design; nothing left to offer.
