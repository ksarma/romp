Under pytest -n 8 two served-lab classes on two workers each ran node esbuild.js into the one dist and copied it; a copy that listed dist during the peer staging phase failed on `.pdf-worker.js.tmp-<pid>-<n>` names renamed away before the copy reached them (shutil.Error at setUpClass; green alone, 11 of 11 iterations red under -n 2 on the two scroll-marks modules). Upstream (67773fb2) carries fifteen served modules in the same build-block shape: the thirteen here (four changed upstream outside the build block: test_gear_select_matrix, test_queued_copy_held, test_ship_reship, test_tab_groups_rows) plus tests/test_notification_tap_resume_browser.py and tests/test_refusal_notice_served.py, which reach the fork through the fold (fork PR #451). An offer converts all fifteen, or its own ratchet fails upstream on the two it left. Upstream also lacks tests/romp_load.py, so an offer carries the tests/__init__.py registration too. Fork-side follow-up, done: #440 merged fork main after #451 landed (merge commit c51146da) and converted the two fold modules in the commit on top of that merge, so the ratchet guards them from the first main that holds both.
