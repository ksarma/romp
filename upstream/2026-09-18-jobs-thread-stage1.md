Upstream ships the same walk: the wake-only mode is its #936 and the parse gate is its T401, which the fork took in the 2026-09-15 pull-in. With the auto-nudge toggle off the walk runs wake-only and a wake-only look neither skipped nor recorded (the toggle was read as not a file of the session), so every alive session paid the state gates, a parse-cache lookup, the awaiting probe, a shared store view and a goal walk on every housekeeping pass: looks equal to parses, 7353 per 120 s on a 39-session box, 42 to 47 ms of an 85 ms pass. The toggle is the nudge ledger, the tenth keyed file, so the wake-only look now records a memo row under its own mode tag (full, wake, or wake plus reminders; a row serves a look of the same mode only) and skips while the key stands and no noted instant has come. Before that, the two blanket None notes that made every stamped and every delegated top unbounded (stampedWait, allDelegated) are retired, since every ending of a stamped wait is a keyed file or the instant the wake notes and the delegated check is pure over the store, and the wake's exits that read no file name their own legs (freshFault, refusedWrite, peerAlive, dormantOwner), a design-review finding; the code review then found two more ledger writes whose refusal went unnoted once stampedWait was gone, the answered wake's record and the moot stamp, and both note refusedWrite now. A skipped look repeats its verdict and writes nothing; the dead-man fires on the first pass at or after its instant; a skipping pass and a full pass over the same world leave the stores, the ledger and the memo rows byte-identical (a test proves it). Counters: skippedParses rises toward looks with the gear off, and memos.nudgeWalk.wakeOnlyRecorded counts the wake-mode rows. Fold ruling A condition 7 (exactly one shared load per alive session with the toggle off) becomes at most one, zero on a skip. Filed 2026-09-18 by the fork's performance session.
