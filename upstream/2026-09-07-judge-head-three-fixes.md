All three are in upstream's code (`parsed_session`, `_PARSE_CACHE`, `rolledUp`, `_materialize_from_log` and `_baseRev` are all on `upstream/main`, 2026-09-07). Fix 1: `parsed_session` pinned the pass frame's parse only on the `_PARSE_CACHE` miss path, so a session whose parse was already cached (every idle session, most of the time) froze nothing under an open frame, and a turn that ended after the pass's first touch was visible to a later stage and invisible to an earlier one; the pin also covers the pusher's tick jobs that call `parsed_session` while a frame is open, and the accepted lag is that such a turn is judged whole in the next pass. Fix 2: both re-derivers skip a rolled-up node, so a child promoted to top level by umbrella dissolution kept its stale done flag, the settle row it appended never materialized, and every rollup appended one more settle row and one more seam; one live store held such a node at `LOG_CAP` (64 settle rows) and republished 0.6 to 1.1 MB per pass. The sweep unrolls each promoted child and its whole subtree and folds each node from its own diary; a top that leaves the rolled-up state with those settle rows folds to open with `settledAt` set, which the card shows as Working while every `settledDone` reader treats it as sealed, so both unroll sites end that episode once with a romp-authored `reopen` row through `record_verdict` and one judge-errors row (`unroll-heal`). Fix 3: `save_goals` popped `_baseRev` on every real publish and nothing restored it, so every save after the first in a multi-save function (`_distill_session` after a mirror title, `_plan_session` several times per pass) took the branch for stores built without `load_goals`: rev+1, no rebase, no no-op check; after a successful publish the object now carries the written revision as its base, and a failed publish leaves the base as it was.

Independent of the perf stack in substance (based on `ft-ready` for stacking only); offer as one fix PR or three. Leaves a stale docstring in J4 (`propagate-load-once`, #266), recorded there. Three lenses plus refuters reviewed the fixes; the one-level unroll and the sealed-Working shape of a healed top were the two should-fix findings, both fixed and pinned.
