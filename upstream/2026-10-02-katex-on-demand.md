KaTeX (about 86 KB served, 348 KB raw) rode inside every bundle that imports the math grammar: on the fork render.js, feed.js, files.js and waiting.js, so every page that hosts the chat or the viewer downloaded and parsed it whether or not it showed a formula, and the default open (shell, chat, feed) carried two copies. The change makes it an on-demand chunk in the pattern of the editor and PDF chunks. math-chunk.ts is its own webview entry and registers KaTeX as globalThis.__rompKatex. math.ts imports no KaTeX (it types the part it calls by shape). Until the engine has loaded, a formula that passes the fill's bounds stays as its placeholder: the TeX as text, in a dim pending dress both sheets give it, a display formula in KaTeX's display box. It is stamped with its fill call's group, so the per-message budget is still charged per message when it renders later, and the fill asks for the chunk once, through a script tag derived from the page's own bundle tag (chunk-url.ts: the same directory and ?v= token, and the tag's nonce, which the VS Code webview's nonce-only policy requires). When the chunk and the two common KaTeX faces have loaded, the settle handlers run and one fill over the document lays out every waiting formula. The chat keeps the reader's line, or the bottom, around that in-place swap: the first line of text at the viewport top that no formula owns, named by its markdown block in the anchor turn and its character offset there with formula text left out in every form (reading-point.ts), since a reply's formulas usually sit in the turn the reader is partway down and keeping that turn's top let them push the text being read down by their growth; the reload record carries the same line, so a record taken over laid-out formulas lands right over waiting ones, also when the turn is above the fresh page's tail window and the deep-link land's keep offset brings it back (whose window build now puts its fork spots on before the landing, as every sync does); a tab left while a formula waits keeps its reader's line, so a swap that lands while the tab is hidden does not move the text when they come back; the viewer holds a Rendered paint whose block has a waiting formula (the romp loader stays up) and paints once the engine is in, so its first paint is its final one; an open's target (a heading, an offset) waits with the paint and lands at it, never spent over the loader. Where a scrollbar takes width (a desktop browser, Playwright's WebKit), the paint that brings the text's overflow narrows the body, and the width hook's repaint, a frame after the landing, seated the place that paint had read at the note's top, undoing the landing for every held open and for any open whose text lands after its first frame, math or none (so at fork main too); the offset's and the heading's landings now note the place they land on. Any other paint of the viewer's body (a failed reload's pane, the editor) ends the hold, so the arrival never paints over it, and the arrival's repaint waits out a press on the card, as a reload's landing does, so a press on an Outline row or a link keeps its click; the arrival leaves a failed load's sources in the viewers' bodies to that repaint, so nothing in the body moves under the press. A comment is not offered across a formula still waiting or showing a failed load's source, whose text the arrival changes. The fill lays a formula out only once the load has settled as a success, and the chunk and the faces are requested after the document's own load event. A load that fails, registers nothing, finds no bundle tag or has not settled within 60 s shows each formula as its source at once, with the reason in its title, and logs one line per failed attempt; nothing waits on a retry, so the chat shows the TeX and the viewer paints. A failed load is retried, and only events arm a retry (renders repeat on every streaming delta and push): the page life's first failure, the pane shim's reconnect (romp:wsup) and the window's online event each set one flag, which does not stack, and the next fill that meets a formula uses it, never the failure's own fill, the arrival's or a viewer's settle repaint, even one a press on the card parks until the release. A chunk that 404s for good therefore costs at most two requests per page life, plus one per reconnect or online event, at any render rate; a page with no bundle tag arms nothing. A page leaving through the reload core (its fire step: persist, then location.reload) is no failed load: WebKit fires the cancelled request's error then, and math.ts lets the attempt's backstop end it instead; on iOS a reload that bypasses that step (the browser's own control, and romp's direct location.reload() calls) still logs one false line on the dying page. Whichever attempt succeeds first, a retry or a chunk or faces landing after the backstop, lays out every formula, the failure's fallbacks included, with the chat's line and the viewer's place kept. A source block with its Copy button counted as a markdown block, so a retry's success put the reader's line about 896 px off (the served retry case, Chromium in Playwright's iPhone 15 context); 079b46bcc fixes that in reading-point.ts, which counts a source block as a formula by its code child, and so also ends the shift by one in the line's block index that a refused display formula with a Copy button above the reader caused. The arrival unwraps and re-applies the comment marks of every view it laid out, so a passage marked while its formula waited is marked whole; a Rendered pick held over rows puts the loader over them in both viewers; and the glossary's term links and the mention chips skip a waiting formula, so a first-mode link and an @ outlast the arrival.

Measured on the fork (the production webview build, served bytes by the kernel's gzip rule): render.js 585,061 to 503,738 bytes, feed.js 428,135 to 344,550, files.js 314,827 to 231,200, waiting.js 329,102 to 245,535; math-chunk.js 85,680. The default open after a deploy drops from 1,867,882 to 1,703,059 bytes (-164,823, -8.8%); an open that shows math pays for the chunk once.

At upstream/main (f4a572008, 2026-09-24) math.ts imports KaTeX statically and chat-md.ts registers the fill; only render.ts imports chat-md.ts, so only render.js carries KaTeX there, one copy per chat open. upstream/main has no md-config.ts, no waiting.ts and none of the fork's bounds, so an offer would be adapted: the chunk, chunk-url.ts and the engine half of math.ts carry over, the group stamp only matters beside the budget, and the chat's arrival handler and the viewer's hold apply where render.ts and file-view.ts have the same paint paths. The served test, tests/test_math_chunk_served.py, uses the fork's test helpers tests/lab_dist.py and tests/lab_ports.py, which upstream/main lacks, so an offer would carry them or adapt the test to upstream's own lab setup. Tier feature: it adds an on-demand chunk and a pending state and fixes nothing broken. The editor and PDF loaders' missing nonce and their pattern's omission of waiting.js are separate candidates, not this entry's.
