Fork CI 2026-09-19: the hidden timeline replay's data row read p50 0.0 in 4 of 131 runs and the strict p50 > 0 went red. The bundle column is a performance.now() difference rounded to 0.1 ms; under the paint hold the timeline view buffers a whole-state frame and returns before draw(), so a skeleton re-push's delivery is a merge of about 0.1 ms, and Chromium's performance.now() moves in 0.1 ms steps in this non-isolated context, so the reading is 0.1 or 0.0 by clock phase. The relaxation sits where the flake is, the hidden entries: there the property is that every delivery was measured, the count (bundleMs.n equals delivered; an untaken or negative reading drops out of n) is the witness and the percentiles are its shape. The two visible entries keep the strict per-type p50 > 0 and the first frame's bundleMs > 0, where the readings are 4.5 to 90 ms and have never fired; the project's copy carries those same two claims on its visible pages, and the hidden entry is fork-only. A fourth replay entry holds the page's clock still across every delivery (replay's new pageInit option installs the stub after the instrument), so every reading is 0.0 on each run, with its own pin that the stub took effect (every delivered type's p50 and max exactly 0, the first frame's delivery 0 when it went alone), and a buildReport unit test pins that a 0 reading counts while -1 and a negative one do not. The strict `> 0` claims on a per-delivery timing in the replay loop are four lines: the two handler readings on every entry (the first content frame's handlerMs, 0.2 to 0.4 ms in 12 of 12 probe runs, two clock steps above the floor, and its type's handlerMs.max) and the bundle column's per-type p50 and first-frame reading on the two visible entries; the hidden entries make none. The frozen entry asserts its timing relations under ROMP_UI_BENCH_TIMING like the other three, except the parse against the bundle reading, which the stub holds at 0 and the entry pins on its own. Tests, docs and the bench tool only.
