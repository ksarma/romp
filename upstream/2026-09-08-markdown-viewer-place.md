The viewer kept the numeric scrollTop across every paint of the body, so the passage under the reader's eye changed whenever the content around it did; three smaller defects of the same viewer ride along. A reload after a session's write that inserted twenty paragraphs above paragraph 40 left paragraph 28 (a 900px pane) or 29 (380px) at the top of the view; the Rendered, Raw, Rendered round trip drifted, since the Raw view is the taller of the two (paragraph 53 on top at the return, at 900px); closing the Comments aside at 900px took the top paragraph from 40 to 73, and a pane drag or a text-size step moved the reader the same way. The notice bar (`#fileview-save-err`) was prepended into the body, so it scrolled away with the text (400px above the body's top at scrollTop 400), went with every swap of the body's children, and the past-the-end notice `scrollToLine` raises was scrolled out of view by the very landing it explains (6732px above the body's top). A pixel-sized picture was squashed at every width: `<img width="900" height="300">` laid out 344 by 300 in a 380px pane and 860 by 300 under the prose measure, the height attribute holding while the max-width cap took the width. The Comment float stayed at its old coordinates after the body scrolled 300px. Now `reader-place.ts` keeps the place in the file's own terms: before every paint of a text view, `readPlace` records the top-visible block (the first top-level block of the Rendered view, or the block holding the first Raw row, whose box ends below the body's top edge), its source span, how far its top edge sits from the body's top, its height and whether the body stood at its top; after the paint, `seatPlace` finds the block in the new view, whichever view it is, and scrolls the body so it sits where the block sat. The place is a block of the lexer's block table (`sourceBlockSpans`, shared with the rendered index; `renderedBlockIndex`, `renderedBlockElements`, `rawRows`, `rawRowSpan`), in Raw the run of rows from its first line to its last, so a blank row between blocks reads as the block after it; a reader partway into a block keeps the depth as a fraction of the block's height across a view switch or a reflow (line 50 of a code block, row 45 of a table, 400px into a figure all come back) and in pixels when the write changed the block's text; a body at its top stays at its top; a hidden element is read around; a floated figure is passed over for the paragraph beside it; and the anchor map's cache checks every child by identity, since the Comments panel wraps a top-level picture in a span while open. A refused block (a table, a code block, an HTML block) is a block like any other, since the place needs an element to measure, not text to quote. Across a reload the span is followed through the edit first (`followPassage`, the comments composer's own follow). renderBody reads the place against the text the body was painted from, swaps, runs the seam's hooks, then seats, the order the text-size change's selection keeper set, so the panel's paint pass has run before the body moves; the seam's reload and setMode, the two view buttons and openUrlView's own renderBody all go through it, and the Files pane and the chat modal come back to paragraph 40 at the same height in every case (pane 900 scrollTops 1918, 2854, 1918). The notice bar is a child of the card between the title bar and the body row (`box.insertBefore(bar2, main)`; `.fileview > .fileview-err { flex: 0 0 auto; }` in both sheets), shows at any scroll position and outlives every swap, and the editor's entry and exit remove it themselves, which the body swap did for them before; the fallback editor's notice goes through the same `noteBar`. `img` joins Slice 1's pixel-sized media rule (`:where(.fileview-md :is(img, svg, canvas, video)[width]:not([width$="%"])) { height: auto; }`, byte-equal in styles.css and feed.css), so the 3:1 picture is 3:1 at 380 and 900px. The float hides on the body's scroll (`hideFloatOnScroll`, a passive listener installed with the float's other listeners so it runs in the list layout as well as the margin layout; the margin lock's mirrored scroll hides it too, since the passage has moved then as well; a scroll that leaves the passage within a pixel of where the button was offered, Chromium's anchoring adjustment when a figure above the viewport lands, keeps it; the same listener runs on a figure's load, since a figure landing inside the viewport above the passage moves it with no scroll event at all); the selection stands and the next mouseup offers the button again. `.fileview-main` drops its `container-type: inline-size`, redundant since fork PR #375 put one on the card.

Five calls in the build depart from the plan's text or go beyond it, each recorded in the plan's Slice 2 build note. The round trip returns to the passage, not the scrollTop the text asks for: one scrollTop is two passages when the Raw view is taller, so the leg asserts the passage at the same height, the Raw scrollTop larger and the Rendered one back. No `overflow-anchor: none`, though the text asks for it: the swap removes the anchor node and Chromium keeps the numeric scrollTop under `auto` and `none` alike (1918 to 1918), and after a seat anchoring helps, since a 200px growth above the kept block (a figure landing late) left the block in place under `auto` (scrollTop 2471 to 2671) and pushed it to paragraph 36 (900px) or 38 (380px) under `none`; the sheets declare nothing and file-view-place.test.ts pins the absence. A block the write rewrote is placed by the block before it, followed the same way, and the block after that is seated: with twenty paragraphs inserted above and the block under the reader's eye rewritten, the common prefix and suffix span both edits, the block reads as gone, and a first cut landed at the edit's start, twenty paragraphs up; with the predecessor rewritten too the block after places it (a section rewritten with a preamble added), with both neighbours gone the nearest standing block before it places it, a deleted block seats the block after it at the edge, and only with none standing does the place fall to where the edit begins. A reader partway into a block that shows lines (any block in Raw, a markdown code block in Rendered, whose first character's top is what is kept) keeps the line at the edge itself, followed through the edit when it stands, so line 50 stays line 50 after lines inserted above it inside the block or deleted below it; a line the write rewrote, or one whose text recurs, is no line to follow and the block's depth rule applies, as it does to an html block's `<pre>`, whose lines and the block's are one off. Where the anchor map's pairing of an html block to several elements is not confirmed by the block's own source parsed with DOMParser (a wrapper the browser nests later markdown into, paired to every element after it; two html blocks a blank line apart, paired as nothing and both), the place reads as none and the seat declines, so the viewer leaves the body's scrollTop alone rather than seating a guess, until the map's flattened walk (Slice 5) pairs them right. The SVG Source view, a text view, reads and seats like the Raw view. The width reflow and a text-size step seat the place too, beyond the text, which names the swap alone: the ResizeObserver reports after the layout has changed, so the place is read off the body's scroll event once per animation frame and the repaint seats the place read before the width moved, and a scroll under a body width the last read did not see is the browser's own (its anchoring adjustment, its clamp) and skipped, unless the seam's aside hook saw the width change made: the hook reads the body's scrollTop after the mount, the browser's adjustment in it, and a scroll reporting another number is a script's after the mount (the Comments panel's reveal, which scrolls on purpose in the same task as the width change; the review found the repaint undoing it) or the reader's, and is read, the clamp excepted; an earlier signature for the anchoring, the kept block's top edge within a pixel of where it stood, missed a table, a list and a blockquote, where the browser anchors inside the block; the text-size step reads before `applyTextSize` and seats after the hooks. One rule that looks simpler was measured and rejected: `height: auto` on every `.fileview-md img` grew `<img width="100%" height="30">` from 30 to 115 and 287px and a height-only badge to the full column, the two guard cases Slice 1's svg and video rule met. The row's `container-type` beside the card's was redundant, not a defect: a size query measures its container's content box, the row is exactly the card's, and the fold's two rules always flipped together (an earlier draft of the note claimed a 2px band of card widths where they disagreed; the review's probe over the base commit's sources found none). The top block is a binary search over the boxes' bottoms that reads around boxes with no layout and passes a floated figure over for the first paragraph beside it ending below the edge, so the scroll-time read costs a handful of box reads per frame.

Not yet offered, and little of it lands on upstream's tree as it is. `reader-place.ts` reads the anchor map (`anchor-map.ts`, which upstream does not ship) and `followPassage` from `file-comments.ts` (likewise), both of the file-comments candidate (2026-09-07-file-comments-tracked-changes, from plans/file-review.md); the width-reflow and text-size seats sit in the ResizeObserver repaint and `setTextSize` of fork PR #348 (2026-09-07-markdown-viewer-text-size), which upstream's file-view.ts lacks along with the seam's `fireRenderedKeepingSelection`; the float's scroll listener is the panel's; and `.fileview-main`, the row whose `container-type` went, is the panel's too.
