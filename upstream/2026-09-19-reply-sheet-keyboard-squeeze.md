On a phone with the keyboard up a todo's long detail filled the Reply sheet and the answer box was one squeezed line above Cancel and Send (2026-09-19, screenshot). The sheet is a column flex box capped at the window with overflow hidden; a wrapped text block's automatic minimum refuses to shrink and a textarea's resolves to zero, so the box took the whole deficit and the cap clipped. Four rules scoped to the dialog (the answer box a fixed item with a three-row floor; the detail the item that gives way, a scroll container scrolling within itself, never under two of its lines, wrapping an unbreakable token, and capped at 12em with the keyboard up and at the larger of 12em and 37% of the window's height at rest, so a tall window shows more of a long detail; the short-window frame on the overlay's own selector; the box scrolling at every height as the backstop) and, in both builders, the picker's kb-tight fold on the window's own resize, a handler that publishes the window's height for the cap's at-rest term and withdraws it while the keyboard is up (read on the parent window as the shell's kbOpen reads it: its visual viewport more than 120px shorter than its layout viewport), plus a grow handler capped at the room the box has left, read from the box, re-run on the resize, and standing down for a height the person dragged (file-comments.ts autosize's guard). The maintainer's round 1 found the first cut's three rules failing in composition at 390x508 (the answer box grown to a share of the window laid Send below the box's clip, and a tap there closed the sheet with the answer): the room cap, the floor, the every-height scroll, the drag guard and the served leg are that round's fixes. Fake-DOM legs execute the fold and the grow out of each builder's source; rule pins read the sheet with comments stripped; browser legs in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit measure the assembled sheet with a tap on Send at 390x508, 420, 490, 900, 300 and 230, and the detail's cap at rest at 844, 900, 1080 and 300 and under the keyboard at 508, against the served /waiting page and the chat's real builder; a served pytest leg drives both served pages, the cap at rest at 900 and 1080 and under the keyboard among them, in CI's "Browser-backed served-page tests (pytest)" step; the two browser legs run in neither of the job's two browser steps (its Test step runs them before the browser install, so they skip there, and they are not on the ci-browser-legs.txt roster).
