The guard on `_revive_postal_bus` adopted at the fold from the project's tip (fork main had no such guard, and `_BUS_ENSURED` with its four lines is the project's text too) read `ROMP_POSTAL_CLIENT_ONLY` alone, one term of the postal service's own authority (`is_client_only()` is peers OFF and, the variable or the marker file), and so was backwards in both directions: with peers on, the default, the variable is inert to the service, so the guard disabled the bus revive for a kernel that did own a bus and never fired for the real client-only host; beside it `_BUS_ENSURED` was armed on any exit-0 `ensure`, the client-only ping of a tunnel included, so `_bus_port`'s stated precondition, a bus of this kernel's own, was not what the flag measured. The corrected shape keeps the environment's word (read as "this kernel must start no bus", stricter than the service's mode on purpose: mirroring `is_client_only()` would let the suite's floor, the word with peers on, spawn a real bus off a refused notify on a daemon thread) and keys the skip on the kernel's own ensured flag, `if no_bus_word and not _BUS_ENSURED[0]: return`, which the ensure runner arms only on the owned roads that `ensure` now reports: `ensure_road()` returns (ok, road) with the roads spawned, up, answering, client-only, refused and down (`up` requires the bus's own port record to name a live pid under this token's mark, so a stale tunnel or another environment's bus answering the port is `answering`, owned by nobody here), the CLI verb prints `ensure: road=<road>` on stdout, `ensure()` keeps its bool contract for the in-process callers, and the kernel reads the line (`_ensure_road`) and arms `_BUS_ENSURED` for `spawned` and `up` alone (postal_service.ENSURE_OWNED, mirrored as the kernel's `_BUS_OWNED_ROADS`), says an unowned road once on stderr, and once armed leaves the flag standing. The refuters' evidence, by execution: the re-cut pin in tests/test_kernel_trust.py (exit 1, and exit 0 on the client-only, answering or unnamed road, arm nothing; spawned and up arm; armed once, the flag stands) and the new tests/test_postal_bus_revive_guard.py (a private state root with session-hosts off, the ensure a stub or its ping stubbed, Popen a recorder, never a real bus: the floor's world skips, a kernel that ensured a bus of its own revives word or no word, a client-only host never revives, the six roads and the verb's stdout line end to end) are red before the fix, 13 cases across the two modules against the fold's merged text, and green after (52), with the served floor's bus census unchanged before and after the run; the fix is a fork divergence of adopted text and an upstream candidate.
