set_auth_default wrote sdk-defaults.json and touched no session, while docs/reference.md promised that a session with no pick of its own takes the new side at its next launch; per-session hosts and their CLIs outlive a kernel restart, so that launch never came and every follower stayed on the old side. The write now walks the live sessions and asks every follower whose CLI runs on the other side (the CLI's own report before the composed stamp, which a re-attach stamps untruthfully; an attach whose CLI has not reported yet stamps nothing, and the CLI's first init decides) to reconnect exactly as a per-session pick asks, with no pick written, so it keeps following the default; every landing of a follower runs the same comparison, so a session with no live object at the write is asked at its attach; the relaunches draw the machine-wide spawn-stagger slot at the arm, with the CLI still serving, and free it at the handshake; a follower's ask survives a kernel restart and is made again at the attach to the surviving CLI, held for the background work that CLI still runs (counted from the registry's mirror at the attach, and decided by the CLI's own turn-end report, the Stop hook's task list, at the first turn after it: a task it lists is confirmed, a shell it omits is retired, any other type it omits is kept until the CLI's own stream ends it (by registry type: the CLI's ordinary Monitor over a shell command registers as a shell and is retired like one; a kept monitor is a monitor_mcp or monitor_ws task), and a running task it names that the kernel never saw is counted); one follower's fault leaves the walk to the rest, through the one guarded entry point every caller of the follower's step shares, and since round 5 the picked closer at the CLI's first init runs as that guard's step too; a per-session pick pending across a re-attach the kernel cannot read is decided by the CLI's first init, served when the CLI bills the pick and asked when it does not (round 4 served alone, and a re-pick of the same side inside the attach window, which arms no reconnect, latched with the session billing the old account; the round 5 addendum: a reconnect another pick holds in its deferred form, behind a turn or for live work, is that pick's alone and no arm for the billing pick, since its withdrawal ends it, so the billing pick is asked beside it and the withdrawal leaves the arm standing for it); the launch and the per-init check read the file's auth only beside authExplicit, so one per-session pick no longer rings on every unpicked session. kernel/kernel.py carries the relaunch slot waiter's thread kind (sdk-slot in _THREAD_KIND_PREFIXES). The spawn's seed and the new-session picker's preselection still read a per-session pick's flag-less value, as before this change: making them read only the explicit default changes which account new sessions bill on a box with a remembered pick, so that change is split out into its own PR (the reviewer's round 2, 2026-09-19).

What a default change costs, with the reviewer's rounds 1 and 2 applied: every follower on the other side relaunches its CLI, staggered three at a time on the boot-resume budget, each at a quiet moment of its own (no turn in flight, no fed text, no live subagent, background task or Workflow run), and it keeps serving until its slot is granted; a follower that carried its ask across a kernel restart holds it for the surviving CLI's background work until the CLI's own stream ends that work or its turn-end report at the first turn after the attach retires it; a turn that ends with no report holds what nothing spoke for and says so (the round 3 pre-check of 2026-09-19; until it, the settle dropped those rows). The standing rule for that mechanism (round 2): nothing is torn down, reported to the session as cut off, or wiped from the registry's mirror on the inference that work ended, only on the CLI's own report; without one the row is held and the log says so. Sharpened in the round 3 pre-check: a report's presence is always trusted, its absence only for the task types the reporter is known to enumerate completely, and one predicate (report_absence_decides, in kernel/sdk_backend.py) answers one question for both reconciles of the Stop hook's list, which types an omission may retire: shells alone, on a probe of 2026-08-28 and a read of the bundled CLI 2.1.266's producer on 2026-09-19 (the list is built from the whole task registry on that build, so it is type-agnostic there; the read is not a probe, and the kernel launches whichever claude the PATH resolves, so the unverified types stay on the restricted side until one probe per type widens the set). The predicate rules the launch ledger's same-generation entries only: a cross-generation entry is tombstoned by the ledger's procGen branch before the predicate runs, so on the survivor re-attach the two reconciles can be decided by different rules (the seeded rows by the predicate, the ledger's stale-generation entries by procGen), and the two key on different spellings of one task (the ledger on the launch tool's name, the seeded side on the registry type), so a Monitor over a shell command is held by the ledger and retired by the seeded side on one payload; harmless, since the live mirror, not the ledger, is the liveness authority. A retry after a handshake that timed out against a surviving CLI retires nothing (round 2's high), and a stand-down after four such timeouts holds the rows and the mirror for the next attach instead of filing a death notice for a CLI still alive (the round 3 pre-check). The Stop hook reads the report before any of its bookkeeping, so a bookkeeping step that raises cannot turn a report into a no-report, and a payload without the list is a no-report, never an empty one. Two residuals are disclosed, not closed, since both are one design question (what the authoritative read of a surviving CLI's live work is at attach time, for background tasks, Task agents, Workflow runs and hook-only subagents alike), which the reviewer has queued: a recorded task whose closing record never reaches the kernel holds the ask, the pending dots and a stoppable-task row until the CLI's own report or stream speaks for it, and for a task other than a shell until its end frame alone, across turns and restarts (a replayed end frame pops the row but releases no pick); a held row also counts in the box's restart-disruption reading (round 4 of the reviewer's review, 2026-09-19), keeping /busy above zero and the update banner's confirm step naming the session, so a quiet deploy waits to its 15-minute backstop, while the automatic converge, which counts in-flight turns alone, is unaffected; and a subagent known only to the SubagentStart hook has no registry mirror and is never counted, so a survivor whose only live work is such a subagent can be re-armed at the attach. The bundled CLI 2.1.266 also pushes an unprompted background_tasks_changed snapshot behind a repeated initialize's success response (settled by execution, 2026-09-19: none behind the first initialize, one behind each repeated one), covering running or pending non-foreground, non-observer tasks only; it arrives after the handshake, so the seed cannot read it, and it is not applied, since under replace semantics it would retire every live foreground task at each survivor attach. One consequence for the user's packet, decided by nothing here: a session spawned while a default stands (the explicit one, or a per-session pick's remembered value) carries it as a pick of its own and is not moved by a later change; whether such a session should follow instead is a semantics question for the user. One residual is named and not closed here, the unapplied-pick family (the reviewer's round 6, 2026-09-19): a pick that cannot be applied, or that loses to something else, and then never resolves visibly, in four faces (a wrong landing's relaunch onto the fallback side; an attach that can tell, with a record differing from the pick; a pending the constructor heals across a kernel restart under a live host lease; the picked branch's third or-arm, reachable only when Claude Code's settings cannot be read or on a key pick with no helper and no login), every face pre-existing on main, this change turning main's wrong served into a standing pending on the attach road, the user's exit a pick of the side the CLI bills, and a queued piece owning the closing rule: a pick that cannot be applied ends in a visible row saying it was not applied and what is billing instead. The kernel side goes live at the next kernel restart of the box that serves the sessions. An offer waits on the user's word.
