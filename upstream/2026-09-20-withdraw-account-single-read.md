Found by the adversarial verification of round five of https://github.com/romp-on/romp/pull/994 at head a3d4d7169 (2026-09-20) and scoped out of that round, because the fix changes what the account says, a contract plans/user-todos.md states, and no row is lost or misstamped through the window. _withdraw_user_todo holds the store lock for its whole section, but the section reads the store three times, and the readers take no lock and stat the file on every call (_user_todos_read: a cache keyed on mtime_ns and size; a stat or read raising an OSError other than FileNotFoundError answers the empty stand-in beside a fault flag). A fault that begins between two of the reads therefore gives one section two verdicts on one file, and the account is worded from the wrong one. Three windows, in the code at that head. First, a fault beginning after _user_todos_unreadable() answered healthy and before the row lookup through _user_todos(): the lookup reads the empty stand-in, finds no row, and the account is state unknown with owner False, the "not yours" wording the check was added to keep off an unreadable store. Second, a fault beginning after the lookup found the row open and before the stamp: _resolve_user_todo reads the stand-in, finds no open row and writes nothing (ok False); the re-read after it finds no row either, so the pre-stamp row stands in, its resolved field empty, and the account is the "malformed closing stamp on the id: resolved=None" error with ok False and owner True, on a healthy open row. Third, a fault beginning at the re-read after a stamp that landed: the same malformed-stamp error, with ok True, while the row on disk carries a well-formed withdrawn stamp. The first and third windows are the round's finding; the second is read from the same code while filing this entry, not executed. Not data loss: every write in the section is refused or complete on the writer's own read, and the row on disk is right in each window; the account for that one call is wrong.

Remedy sketched: the section makes one _user_todos_read() call and branches everything on that (store, flag) pair, the unreadable account on the flag and the row lookup on the store; _resolve_user_todo returns the stamp it wrote (the {kind, t} dict, None when it stamped nothing) so the account is worded from the write's own result and no read follows the stamp. _resolve_user_todo's own read is then the one read left inside the section besides the first: either it takes the caller's (store, flag) in place of reading, or a no-stamp return under a flagged read is worded as the unreadable account, which is sound because every writer of the store holds the lock (all four _write_user_todos callers at that head sit under it), so a row the section found open cannot have been closed by another writer before the stamp, and a missing stamp after an open find can only be the store's fault. Tier fix, with a test that fails before it: a stat fault that begins after the section's first read (the counted-call shape of the stat fault WriterCarriesItsOwnVerdict in tests/test_user_todos.py uses, aimed at the section's later stats), asserting the unreadable account (state unknown, owner None, error naming the store) for the first two windows and, for the third, an account carrying the stamp the call wrote (ok True, state withdrawn, at its time). The fix builds on _user_todos_read and _user_todos_unreadable, which the fork's main does not carry yet (both are that PR's), so it is built once the PR merges and folds, or on the offer branch on the maintainers' word. The plan changes with it: plans/user-todos.md says a withdrawal against an unreadable store is told the store could not be read (owner null, never "no such request"); the fix makes that hold for a fault beginning at any point of the section, and the function's docstring sentence about one critical section should say one read as well.
