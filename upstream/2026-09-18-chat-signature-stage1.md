Upstream ships the same pusher and the same chat signature pass, which pays about 4 ms of wall per tab per cycle with a dashboard attached and had no counter for what it does. Stage 1 of the chat-signature design is measurement with zero behaviour change: GET /perf memos.chatSig counts the signatures taken (pre, post, thread, nosig, waited), the chat builds that raised past a signature and the targeted push's builds (failedBuilds, targetedBuilds: at rest pre = builds.chat cached + built - targetedBuilds + failedBuilds, and over a window with failedBuilds zero post = built - targetedBuilds - nosig), the cache reads that met a cached entry with a signature in hand and the components of their operands equal by object identity (compares, compareIdenticalComponents; the share is compareIdenticalComponents over compares times the 40 labels of _CHAT_SIG_LABELS, never over compares alone), the os.stat, os.lstat and DirEntry.stat calls made while a signature is open whichever module makes them (stats), the names, switch and registry reads inside a signature (namesReads, switchReads, regReads), a warm-tab census (warmEligible, warmBlockedByOutline, heldBody) and the per-push denominator (pushes, every push that ran the chat tab loop, connect pushes included); stages_cpu_ms carries each chat seam and container thread CPU (user, sys) from getrusage(RUSAGE_THREAD) beside the wall, empty where the platform lacks the clock, and keeps the CPU for the flat rows alone (a mark whose wall the stage-attribution fix routes to pusher.cycleJobsMs, pusher.connectPush.stagesMs or stagesForeign records no CPU row, a push-marked write from a thread owning no cycle included); and push.chat.sig becomes a container of push.chat.sig.static and push.chat.sig.deps under the nested-sum rule of PR 759, the signature's bytes in the cycle split on the static row. Every frame is byte-identical with the counting sites as no-ops, proved by the six-cycle wire test. Filed 2026-09-18 by the fork performance session.

stats is counted at a choke point, not per site: the kernel wraps os.stat and os.lstat at import on the os and posix modules (and on pathlib's accessor on Python 3.10), every os.path, pathlib and importlib caller reaches the wrappers, and every scandir entry's stat in kernel/ goes through _entry_stat or one of its twins in the other kernel modules (a source pin derives the entry names from every scandir there), since a DirEntry stats in C and reaches no wrapper; the fstat inside open() and inside scandir(), a DirEntry predicate on a symlink entry or on a filesystem without d_type, and the stats of the git children a cold cwd memo forks are not counted, because no Python function makes them. A test intercepts the three in-process around real signatures over a real state root and asserts the counter equals the interception count; at the round-1 head, which counted at eight named sites, the counter saw about 7 of 23 stats per signature.
