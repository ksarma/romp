Stage 0 of the reconnect design (client half). On the phone layout only the chat, the feed (exempt: the bell) and the stored tab load at boot; the Outline, the Sessions band and the Waiting pane load on their first tap, with the src set before the re-tell and a shell-owned loader until the iframe load event. The phone chat pane first dial declares skeleton=1, so a cold open is served one full plus statuses; the idle prefetch chain starts from the stored tab first full applied (or from the strip when it lists no such tab) instead of the strip first paint; an off-screen feed on the phone applies its first frame without painting the board. Desktop unchanged. The Files pane stays eager: its markup line is upstream text (a one-line follow-up). Fails-before tests T1 to T6 per the stage-0 design. Offer waits on the user word.
