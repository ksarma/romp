Slice 3 of plans/markdown-viewer.md (decisions 3, 4 and 5 ruled 2026-09-07); the build note under the slice records where the code departs from the plan text. Depends on Slices 1 and 2 (fork PR #390, 2026-09-07-markdown-viewer-sanitizer, and the mdviewer-s2 branch, 2026-09-08-markdown-viewer-place) and on fork PR #348 (2026-09-07-markdown-viewer-text-size, a candidate, not offered): the prose size is `calc(var(--fs) * 1.15 * var(--fv-scale, 1))`, so the column decision 4 rules scales with #348's text-size control; the typescale leg presses that control (`button[aria-label="Larger text"]`) and waits for `--fv-scale` to read 1.15 before it measures the headings and the column at 115 percent; and the slice removes #348's 860px block cap and the media cap that scaled with it and rewrites the pins in #348's own file-view-text-size.test.ts. A Slice 2 landing does not carry #348 with it (that entry allows a port that drops its two #348 seats), so this one names it. The sheets alone fall back to 100 percent without it (`var(--fv-scale, 1)`), and a port over upstream's pre-#348 `.fileview-md` writes the new rule in place of upstream's and has no 860px cap to remove; the leg's two stepUp calls have no button to press there.
