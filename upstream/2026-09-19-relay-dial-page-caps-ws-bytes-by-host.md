Part 1 is the remote client half of the reconnect design: a remote redial declares the pair its conn's own bases hold, held:feed:<gen>.<rev> from the pair beside the raw feed base and held:bars:<gen>.<rev> from the receiver's bars base, beside the decoder word feedDelta and never from the page's caps string (the page's held member is its LOCAL kernel's pair, and its hold words readyGate and chatResume are holds on its own socket). connect()'s per-dial reset is gated per base, so a base a stamping kernel left a generation on survives the redial and a base holding none is reset as before; the bars receiver accepts a frame carrying through at any rev at or above its base with rev equal to through, the caught-up resume included, and refuses a stamped patch whose gen is not its base's as the feed road does (a stamped patch onto a base holding no gen applies and seeds none; the receiver says why the roads differ there); a stamped feedDelta applies under a gen gate (gen equal, base at or below the held rev, its through, which every stamped delta carries, at or above it, and its rev equal to its through, so the pair advances to the one rev the frame states) and a refused one asks for the full carrying the held pair, while a feedDelta carrying no gen applies on the base's presence alone and moves no pair, the vintage guard revision 11 of the design states as its clause (2). A feedDelta whose apply THROWS (a malformed frame: asks not a list, a null item, removeAsks not iterable) is refused on both roads since the maintainer's round 5 (the throw caught once, in feed-delta.ts, so both callers are covered by construction): nothing is written, the base and the pair stand, one bare needFullFeed goes per stall, a feedDelta-apply row names the road (wire or local) and the word (asked, or stopped once a second throw follows the answering full, when the asking stops and the shell is told once), and a delta that applies clears the bound; the remote latch lives on the conn and resets with its socket, the local one on the manager for the page's life. A gen is the kernel's string (its boot token and a counter joined by '-', holding neither '.' nor ',', at most 64 characters: a dial URL carries one gen per held member for the conn's life and the relay refuses a request line over 65,536 bytes); a present value of any other form is one the client cannot read, and unparseable is not absent: a full carrying it seeds no pair, and a delta carrying it, or a composed frame whose newGen is one, onto a base holding a pair is refused on both roads (the feed road with the field's word, gen or newGen, and the held pair on the ask; the bars receiver into needSlot), never applied as a gen-less frame and never left under the old gen; onto a base holding no gen a delta carrying one applies as a gen-less delta does.
