Upstream ships the same viewer, the same Comments panel and the same anchor map, so every item applies there once the slices before it have landed: a selection made in the Rendered view inside a table cell or a code line maps to the source and its highlight lands on the selected occurrence, where before both refused and the person commented from the Raw view; a selection across two cells is refused with the reason named and Switch to Raw preselects the exact span, so Save works from there; a session's change inside a cell or a line paints on that cell or line by position; a comment on a display formula shows over the whole formula and its card offers Scroll instead of Reveal; and a comment made from Rendered on a cell written with an escaped pipe, or over a tab-indented code line, stores the source's bytes.
