Two defects the user sees on both platforms, visible on the phone: typing and streaming stutter (in compact mode, the default, every streamed frame that changed or appended an event rebuilt the whole rendered window, at least 80 units, once per animation frame) and the head spacer jumping 24k to 1.43M px in one second (the per-turn estimate was the window's whole height over its user-row count, measured once for the view's life, so a tail window with one user row read about 7,150 px per turn). Fix 1: the compact tail trims by data-unit from the first unit the plan names (chat-compact-tail.ts) and re-appends with appendItem; the rebuild stays for a stale view, a change above the window, a gap past the start or a bottom spacer; the folded-reply footer is patched by position instead of marking the view stale. Fix 2: the median over the turns the window holds whole, at least two, re-measured on every window build, capped at 20 default turns under every reader. Fix 3: no layout read in the render task: heights come from the unit ResizeObserver's border-box map at frame end, the figures reach the DOM inside the next paint, and the spacer diag row reads the scroller a frame later. One change lies outside the three, forced by the second: a follow-mode reader's re-window at the tail (virtualizeToViewport) writes the bottom and arms the view observer to follow the rebuilt rows' first settling (scroll-keep.ts followRebuiltTail), because under the median estimate the rebuilt view came out longer and the go-to-the-live-tail jump ended above the bottom in both engines. Review round 1 added: the trim and the measure key on what is a unit (a hover's rail band, appended as the thread's last child, neither ends the trim nor counts as a row); a tool-run toggle keeps the reader's place as appendActive does (the anchor row put back over the re-sized head spacer); an eviction re-seeds the unit it promotes to the window's first with the build's rail reference; the per-turn median at an even count is the lower middle turn; and a node-level differential compares the incremental tail's DOM with a rebuild of the same state, driven per seam outcome (the append by unit kind and fold state, the spacer branch in both directions below a browsed window, the status-only fast path over every state the stream delivers, the rebuild by the shrink; the rebuild's other whys share one executor with the rebuild leg and have no frame). Review round 2 added: landActive takes before its attempt and its saved-place fallback restores the row captured at the saved place instead of writing the pre-resize scrollTop raw; the kernel's chat allowlist admits the inactive spacer row's marker (view: inactive), pinned by a dispatch assertion; normal mode's tail trims through the shared walk; the hover's rings and glow come off with the band in both tail paths; tailLabel and unitChanges read the tail by the unit predicate when one is given; the build and sync censuses are keyed on the caller and the road, and the fill's no-unit fallback passes its own predicate; keepPlaceAcrossWindow restores the row under the viewport top when both its restores miss.
