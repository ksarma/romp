Every return to the app logged "Kernel connection lost" for the chat and the Feed and left an unread digit on the Log control, although the sockets came back at once. The Log wrote its entry at each pane's up-to-down transition, and every return makes one: the shim puts a stale socket down before it redials, and a thawed page receives the FIN of a socket the OS dropped. Now the transition records the drop in the Log's lost, and the entry is written when the drop's reconnect fails: the close of a dial that never opened, the pane's own (the shim's new wsFail word, posted after that close's down word) or, since the panes wait on the shell's link at a return, the shell's own (window.__rompLinkFailed from its socket's close). A socket that reopens first drops the waiting entry unwritten. No timer decides it: a hung dial fails at its connect cut, so a hung outage is written at 15 s, while the live red cue shows the drop from the start. A pane not shown keeps its drop waiting and is written at a failure once it is shown; a parked pane's drop is dropped. Upstream's main writes the entry at the same transition (read 2026-10-03), so a return there logs it too; upstream has no shell link, so its port is the shim's word and the Log's lost, without the shell's call.
