Since the v0.16.0 tidy every menu opens through ctx-menu.ts's showMenuCard, which focuses the card at the open and, at the close, hands the focus back to the opener only while that node is still connected. The tab menu's opener is the right-clicked tab, and renderTabs swaps every tab node on a kernel push, so a push that lands while the tab menu is open detaches the opener: Escape (or any other dismissal) then leaves the focus on the card, which falls to the body as the card is removed, and the strip's arrow-key session switching (onTabKey) is dead until a click. Before the tidy the hand-built menu never took the focus, so the tab kept it and renderTabs's own focus restore carried it across the rebuild. Reproduced in Chromium by the fork's 2026-09-17 catch-up review (a playwright probe on the bundled builder: the pre-tidy code refocused and ArrowRight switched, the post-tidy code left the body focused). The fix is in the tab menu's close callback, onTabMenuClosed: after its forgets (three in the fork's callback, two in upstream's), when the document has the focus and the card still holds it (closeContextMenu runs onClose before it removes the card, so a check for the body alone fails) or the focus already fell to the body, focusActiveTab({ preventScroll: true }) runs (the tab, else its folded section's head; focusActiveTab takes an optional FocusOptions threaded to both rungs, and only this caller passes preventScroll, so a press on the strip that dismisses the menu does not scroll the bar under the pointer before the click lands: the review's round 2, item 1); a standing opener already has the focus back from the builder and is left alone, and a close under the window's blur or after the focus left for a field elsewhere moves nothing. The selection menu, which shared the callback, gets its own plain onSelectionMenuClosed (ctxMenuEl = null): a right-click on transcript text has no opener and its close must not pull the focus into the strip. Executed cases in ui/webview/ctx-menu.test.ts on the shim's nodes with the real showMenuCard and the callbacks lifted from render.ts: the rebuilt-strip Escape lands the focus on the active tab's fresh node and ArrowRight switches (red before the fix: the focus on the body, no switch); the plain close leaves the body focused; a standing opener keeps the focus; a blur moves nothing. Upstream's render.ts at f4daa02e2 carries the same two calls and a two-line callback (ctxMenuEl and tagsFlyNewInput forgotten; the fork's adds its views hook line, tabMenuViewsHook, the fork's Hide-tab layer), so the code change, the two refocus lines and focusActiveTab's optional FocusOptions parameter, applies there unchanged; of the pins, only ctx-menu.test.ts's exact-body pin needs re-aiming to upstream's shorter body in an offer (tab-hide.test.ts is fork-only and stays out; the lift's prelude runs unchanged; upstream's own readers of focusActiveTab's signature and second rung, ui/webview/composer-send.test.ts near 60 and ui/webview/tab-groups.test.ts near 277 to 278 (its second-rung pin; the fork's own copy of that pin near 292 is not upstream's), re-aim to the parameter as the fork's did in its review round 2). Filed by the fork's 2026-09-17 catch-up fold (review round 1 of its pull-in PR, correctness-1); the offer waits on the no-new-upstreaming hold like the 2026-09-16 entries.
