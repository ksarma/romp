On a phone the Log's filter toggles ran past the panel and off the screen. The grid's columns were repeat(5,1fr): a 1fr track's minimum is its chips' min-content width and the chips never wrap, so the five columns stayed 341px (WebKit, Firefox) to 362px (Chromium) wide. In the phone's min(700px, 94vw) panel, in WebKit and Firefox, they ran past the filter bar's content edge below 414px of viewport, past the panel below 401px and off the screen below 388 to 389px; in Chromium below 435, 422 and 409px. At 390px the last column ended 22 to 43px past the filter bar, and at 320px three toggles could not be tapped at all. The columns are now repeat(auto-fill,minmax(max(var(--rerr-chip-col),20% - 5px),1fr)): as many equal columns as fit at 96px and never more than five, so the desktop keeps its five equal columns and a phone gets two below 368px, three from 368px, four from 475px and five from 583px. The 96px is the entry rows' chip column, and both rules read it from one custom property, --rerr-chip-col, declared once on #rerr-panel, so a chip label too wide for it is fixed in one place and the two widths cannot drift apart; a pin in tests/test_error_center.py reads the parsed sheet for that. A served test measures every toggle in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox at nine phone widths, each at 844 and 568px high, and at two desktop widths: the column count at each phone width, every toggle inside the panel and the screen both ways and holding its label, every label within the 96px floor, and at least 97px of list under the grid, room for three one-line entries. Against the old rule it fails at every phone width from 320 to 475px in all three engines.

upstream/main (last updated 2026-09-24) has the same rule: its kernel/kernel.py styles #rerr-fgrid as repeat(5,1fr) in the same panel and filter bar, over 14 kinds (no frozen kind, and refused reads "not saved"), and its tests/test_error_center.py pins that text. By the chip widths measured here, "not saved" among them, its five columns need about 335px (WebKit, Firefox) and 351px (Chromium) where a 390px phone leaves the grid 319px, so it overflows the same way (not run against that tree). The kernel change applies there as it is (its entry rows' rule is the same 96px 1fr auto auto). The served test reads the kinds from the grid, so it needs no change for the 14, but it takes its port and readiness wait from tests/lab_ports.py and its bundles from tests/lab_dist.py, which upstream does not have yet (upstream/2026-10-02-tests-served-lab-ports.md), so an offer carries those calls in upstream's own form. The one-property pin reads the sheet through tests/served_css.py, which upstream does not have either (upstream/2026-09-19-iosperf-kb-gap.md), so an offer carries that pin in upstream's own form too.
