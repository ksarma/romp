The file viewer's picture box and the chat's preview box each carry a data mark, and the chat page's markdown-image heal skips a picture inside either mark instead of inside either box's class. An author can type a class in a message or a note, and the sanitizer keeps classes and strips every data-* attribute, so a figure wrapped in markup carrying either class, or carrying one itself, now parks and heals like any other markdown image, while the preview box's own swirl and pictures stay the preview's. A picture still loading when the Source toggle is pressed runs no paint hooks at its load, and a press from the picture back to the Source view while a reload that landed under it still decodes runs its own decode of the landed bytes and paints at that decode instead of painting the older XML; the reload's own decode then paints nothing. A landing of another type while the Source view is up or on its way paints its own picture with no XML over it. Neither runs a paint hook at the landed mtime over older bytes. The viewer's seam gains onReplaced, told as the viewer puts up a picture or a PDF frame, the romp loader of a re-ask or the PDF pages' loader; its doc says which of those paint at once. The Comments panel's change cards take their state by one rule, stated once, with a table of the roads it was checked on, in the doc of the Panel's #cardState in ui/webview/file-comments.ts; the panel's other comments, its pins and this entry point there. While the body does not show the view's last content paint, a click on a change card's link, or on an insertion's or a substitution's Comment on this change, now says in a row under the card that the change is not in view and does nothing else (notInView); the row shows only under its card, and goes at the next content paint or at a status that no longer lists the change or lists it detached. The row has a slot of its own beside the one a decision's refusal uses, so it never replaces a refusal standing under the card, and the panel's one live region, visually hidden, is emptied at the click and given the same words on the next animation frame (the technique used so screen readers announce repeated text; the announcement itself was not measured with a screen reader). Comments that said the sanitizer keeps data-* attributes now say it strips an author's. This entry is stacked on the entry for the svg picture's /file address (upstream/2026-09-24-svg-picture-file-address.md, fork PR 912), which goes first.
