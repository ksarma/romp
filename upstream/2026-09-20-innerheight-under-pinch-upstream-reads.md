All four lines are the project's (present at the merge base and on the project's main), so the fork does not patch them: a fix at the source reaches every fold, and an override would be permanent cost for a benefit nobody has confirmed reachable.

First filed (2026-09-20) with two reads, the keyboard-open test and the bell popover's bottom. Corrected 2026-09-21 in the branch of fork PR 858, after the maintainer's fifth review round of that PR found the two spend reads named in no body, ledger or comment: every `innerHeight` read in `kernel/kernel.py` was listed (`grep -n innerHeight kernel/kernel.py`, comment lines set aside) and each classified by its context on three questions: a layout-viewport stand-in or not, reached by the phone by design or not, wrong under a standing pinch in the WebKit model or not. Nine reads. Four are upstream stand-ins on a surface the phone reaches by design and wrong under a WebKit pinch: the two first found, plus the spend panel's list cap (`spSizePane`: the spend card opens from the usage panel's By session button, the usage panel from the phone tab bar's Usage button; under a WebKit pinch the cap follows the visual viewport and under-sizes the list toward its 120 px floor while the card itself never scrolls) and the spend chart's tooltip flip (`spTipPlace`: a finger moving over the chart, a pointer move rather than a tap; under a WebKit pinch it misplaces a fixed box, the bell popover's shape). Two more are stand-ins whose phone reachability is unverified, so they are not counted: the timeline bottom band's auto-fit cap (`cap()`, 0.7 of `innerHeight`, gated by `body.po-timeline`, the desktop rail's Timeline toggle; the phone layout shows the timeline as a tab pane, another surface) and the drift banner's drag clamp (`clampXY` in `_STALE_JS`, run on a pointer drag of the banner and on resize). One is a zero test (`paneHidden`, `innerHeight === 0`), no height read. The remaining two are fork PR 858's: upstream's fine-pointer `h` in `fit()` is re-read by the fork line after it as `document.documentElement.clientHeight`, so its value is not used where `clientHeight` exists, and that fork line's own `innerHeight` fallback is reached only where a document element has no `clientHeight` (a node stub).

Evidence: WebKit's LocalDOMWindow::innerHeight returns the unobscured content rect's height, which on iOS is the delegated unobscured size WebPage::updateVisibleContentRects sets from the visual viewport (WebKit source read 2026-09-20); Chromium keeps innerHeight at the layout viewport under a page scale of 2 (an iPhone 14 descriptor: visualViewport.height 422, innerHeight 844, documentElement.clientHeight 844). Headless Linux WebKit refuses a page scale above 1, so the WebKit half rests on the source alone; the on-device read of innerHeight and documentElement.clientHeight under a pinch is the only real-engine confirmation and is missing. The keyboard-gap change (fork PR 858) reads documentElement.clientHeight on its own roads and states the same premise once in its fit() comment. The signal that closes this entry: a device read showing a pinch reachable under the meta, at which point the fork's override becomes worth its cost, or a project change at the four lines.
