Since b0fabb0a7 (T386 stage 2, round eleven) _send_chat_locked withholds every chat frame from a socket until it declares its wire by a ready message or a reconnect=1&proto dial term. A hub page older than the federation's remote ready (f7a80efee) relays to a newer kernel with no proto term and never posts ready on the relay socket, and a pane shim older than the redial's proto term redials after a restart with reconnect=1 alone; both were held silent for the socket's life, strips flowing and every session body withheld (an older dashboard attached to a newer kernel listed the remote's tabs with nothing behind them, 2026-09-18: two relay sockets, 965 and 644 chat frames withheld, chatWithheld rows on the record). The fix is event-based and inside the gate: _implicit_handshake, called first in _dispatch_ws, takes the FIRST client frame from an unhandshaken chat socket of OLDER VINTAGE as a proto-1 handshake, stamps proto 1, wakes the pusher, says so once on stderr and files one kernel-surface client-diag row (what implicitHandshake). Vintage is read from the dial, never from the frame (review round 1): the socket is ready from accept (not held under readyGate, whose shim flushes queued clientDiag rows before the bundle's ready), not the VS Code extension host's pipe (client=ext, stamped at accept; every ext pipe forwards its webview's own ready and replays intents before it on a reconnect), and carries no namespaced iid (the colon-joined iid only a current hub's relay sends since 8fe70da07); neither delta nor reconnect is read, since the older shim's redial carries both. The first cut took the first frame of any unhandshaken socket, and a current hub's federation flushed pending frames before its ready on every relay open, so a proto-2 page's relay was pinned to proto 1 for that window and served an index frame and a turn-0 build: the race b0fabb0a7 closed, reopened for remote sessions. In the same change federation.ts posts the ready before the flush on a non-redial open, so no kernel of any vintage reads a flushed setting as the socket's first word. A real ready after the implicit handshake re-declares the wire, resets the base and pops the mark; a socket that sends nothing keeps upstream's behaviour (no chat frame, the chatWithheld row at close). Tests: the decision table with every cell (tests/test_chat_window_spans.py) and the real Handler._ws driven over the older hub's bare relay, the older shim's redial, a current hub's namespaced relay on both frame orders, the extension's pipe with a replayed intent, the drive-arm order and the ready-after-implicit transition (tests/test_chat_pages.py, RealArm); the page-side order (ui/webview/federation-skeleton.test.ts). docs/read-side.md corrected: it said an unannounced socket is served from accept, false for chat frames since b0fabb0a7.
