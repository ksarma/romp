Since b0fabb0a7 (T386 stage 2, round eleven) _send_chat_locked withholds every chat frame from a socket until it declares its wire by a ready message or a reconnect=1&proto dial term. A hub page older than the federation's remote ready (b84f716a8 for a proto-2 page, f7a80efee for every page) relays to a newer kernel with no proto term and never posts ready on the relay socket, and an upstream pane shim between 8610b8954 and 42ab10dd1 redials after a restart with reconnect=1 (8610b8954's term), no proto term (42ab10dd1's), no caps term and no ready re-posted at its open (no upstream shim of any vintage re-posts one); the gate withheld every chat frame from both, strips flowing and every session body withheld (an older dashboard attached to a newer kernel listed the remote's tabs with nothing behind them, 2026-09-18: two relay sockets, 965 and 644 chat frames withheld, chatWithheld rows on the record). The line tells the shims apart, not their age: a fork pane shim carrying a3a9e7385's re-send announces readyGate and then re-posts a bare ready in its redial's onopen right after its flush, so it declares proto 1 itself one frame later and is declined for that readyGate (review round 3); fd95b435a, the fork merge in the bug report, is an instance of the fork case and newer than 7390404be by wall clock. The fix is event-based and inside the gate: _implicit_handshake, called first in _dispatch_ws, takes the FIRST client frame from an unhandshaken chat socket of OLDER VINTAGE as a proto-1 handshake, stamps proto 1, wakes the pusher, says so once on stderr and files one kernel-surface client-diag row (what implicitHandshake). Vintage is read from the dial, never from the frame (review round 1): the socket announced no readyGate hold (read from caps, never from the effective ready flag a reconnect=1 dial sets: a kernel-served pane's shim flushes queued clientDiag rows before the ready it posts itself, and on a redial re-posts a bare ready right behind them; review round 3) and is ready from accept, not the VS Code extension host's pipe (client=ext, stamped at accept; every ext pipe forwards its webview's own ready and replays intents before it on a reconnect), and carries no namespaced iid (the colon-joined iid only a current hub's relay sends since 8fe70da07); neither delta nor reconnect is read, since the older shim's redial carries both. The first cut took the first frame of any unhandshaken socket, and a current hub's federation flushed pending frames before its ready on every relay open, so a proto-2 page's relay was pinned to proto 1 for that window and served an index frame and a turn-0 build: the race b0fabb0a7 closed, reopened for remote sessions. In the same change federation.ts posts the ready before the flush on a non-redial open, so no kernel reads a current page's flushed setting as the socket's first word, and drops a held needFull the ready's connect push answers instead of flushing it (every held ask on a dial with no skeleton term, the active tab's alone on a skeleton dial). Two intermediate vintages are still taken and repair at their ready: a hub between b84f716a8 and 8fe70da07 (a bare relay dial, its ready posted behind its flush) and a VS Code extension host between a6c57f42a and f19808d19 (no client=ext term); the ready re-declares the wire, resets the base and pops the mark, and the implicitHandshake row records the window. The record, the stderr line and the row quote the frame's type as a word from WS_OPS, the kernel's own list of the ops it accepts, or as `other`, never the client's text (review round 2); WS_OPS is pinned equal to the names the dispatch and the drive compare a type against, read by AST so an arm in any spelling is counted (review round 3). A real ready after the implicit handshake re-declares the wire, resets the base and pops the mark; a socket that sends nothing keeps upstream's behaviour (no chat frame, the chatWithheld row at close). Tests: the decision table with every cell (tests/test_chat_window_spans.py) and the real Handler._ws driven over the older hub's bare relay, the silent upstream shim's redial (no caps term: taken), the fork shim's redial (readyGate announced: declined, then served at its own bare ready), a current hub's namespaced relay on both frame orders, the extension's pipe with a replayed intent, the drive-arm order and the ready-after-implicit transition (tests/test_chat_pages.py, RealArm); the page-side order (ui/webview/federation-skeleton.test.ts). docs/read-side.md corrected: it said an unannounced socket is served from accept, false for chat frames since b0fabb0a7.
