The agent-file lookup's memo (_subagent_file) re-validated a hit by stat-ing every directory its original walk read, which for a nested or missing agent's file is the whole subagents tree; _awaiting_nest resolves every awaiting agent's file per _session_awaiting call, and the pusher makes up to five such calls per session per cycle, the jobs pass one more. So with nothing changed every read re-stat'd the tree's directories once per agent beside the tree's own validation (the cost before the fix and with it, road by road: _subagent_tree_memo_report's docstring). The motivation, a dated reading: measured on two deployed kernels on 2026-09-19, a 20 s py-spy profile of one had _dir_stamp's one os.stat as the top self frame, 28 percent of the samples by the profile's reading, over trees of 305, 383 and 72 directories; the other's memos.subagentTree counters showed 24.5 million validation lstats and 22.7 minutes in validateMs over 6.8 hours across 1,294 directories.

The fix keys the validation on the event a time window would have stood in for: the pusher cycle and the jobs pass. Each opens a thread-confined scope on _live_scope (_subagent_scope_open inside the try beside the other per-cycle slots, closed in the finally) holding three memos: the validated (directories, stats) pair per root, each directory's stamp in the shape _dir_stamp answers, and _awaiting_nest's per-agent launch ids keyed (transcript, agentId). The first reader of a tree in the cycle validates or walks it; every later reader on that thread in the cycle is served it, the agent-file lookup's re-check of its directories included. Each scope entry carries the root it depends on (the tree's own root for its pair and the stamps indexed from it; for a launch fold, the root _subagent_scope's docstring names) and the _SUBAGENT_TREES_GEN value read before its disk read; a root leaving _SUBAGENT_TREES (the forget, per evicted root; the missing-root and replaced-root pops when they removed an entry) moves the gen by one and records the new value against that root in _SUBAGENT_ROOT_EVICTED, and an entry is served only while no eviction of its root has happened since, so an eviction is honoured on both threads for that root's pair, stamps and launch folds and nothing else moves (since 2026-09-21; until then one process-wide value emptied every scope's three maps on any root's eviction, which re-validated every held tree and re-folded every awaiting agent's file on both threads, once per forget rather than once per cycle). The two pops run only on a read the scope does not serve: a root held earlier in the cycle is served until its scope ends (the stale window below), its removal or replacement found by the next cycle's first read on that thread, or by a read with no hold on it (the other loop's first read of it in its own cycle, a handler thread's), whose pop records the eviction so that the holder's next lookup drops the pair. A stamp _dir_stamp took itself, whose tree the scope cannot name (the project directory on an agent-file miss), is vouched by no root and re-taken after any eviction. The table is bounded at 1024 roots; an eviction of a root not in a full table clears it and records the generation, which drops every held entry once. Nothing failed is held: a missing or replaced root, a walk with a failed listing or child lstat, a stamp whose stat raises and a launch fold that did not read the file are answered as before and leave no entry in the scope, and a root whose lstat fails for a reason other than absence (EACCES from a parent, EIO) is neither absent nor held: where the branch took every OSError for absence (it popped the entry, recorded an eviction and noted the tree absent), its readers now answer their standing entries unheld or an unreadable marker and the chat build is told to read again, except that with no entry standing the sidecar map answers {} and the agent-file lookup answers a caller that passes no faults list None, so the viewer says the agent's transcript is missing while the fault lasts (ViewerUnderAnUnreadableTree; having the viewer state the fault is a follow-up fix after #882), and the agent-file walk excludes that tree from its search and nothing else, answering a file found under any other tree, the shape stated once in _subagent_tree's docstring and executed at both edges by tests/test_subagent_tree_memo.py UnreadableRoot (an EIO by mock, a real EACCES), the exclusion by FaultExcludesItsOwnTree beside it; a fault on any other read the walk makes of a place (a place below a tree's root, a candidate file, a project-directory entry, the listing) excludes that place alone, the places stated once in _subagent_file_walk's docstring (FaultBelowTheRoot and FaultOnTheWalksOwnRead); the faulted fold is held for the one _session_awaiting call that observed it (what a faulting fold costs: _subagent_tree_memo_report's docstring) and is folded again by the next call, so a file that became readable is seen then. _dir_stamp now counts each successful os.stat into dirStats, so the counter reads both validators (the lstat half alone before), and a figure from before the deploy and one from after are not one series. The key a chat build records for a subagents tree the agent-file miss walk looked through (_subagent_file_walk) is the served read's (mtime, size) per directory (_subagent_tree_dep_note, the shape the sidecar map already recorded for the own tree), never a stat taken after it: round 1 of #882 found the walk recording each sibling root under a fresh stat taken after the served listing, so a file landing after the hold under a directory the listing lacked was recorded under its own post-landing key, equal to every later re-stat, and the tab that showed the agent's file missing was never rebuilt; and it recorded the root alone, which a landing under a listed child never moves.

Bound per cycle and per pass, for the two loops' own reads: derived road by road, with the executed case that pins each term, in _subagent_tree_memo_report's docstring. Stale window: a change on disk after the cycle's validation is seen by the next cycle's first reader, one cycle later at most (the pusher's wake or its 0.5 s backstop under the 1 s minimum interval, the jobs thread's 0.5 s pace, plus the cycle's own duration), the tree's own removal or replacement included (the pops run on a read the scope does not serve).

tests/test_subagent_tree_stamps_per_cycle.py: a tree of eight directories with three nested workflow agents beside a synthetic session, driven through the real _pusher_cycle and _jobs_cycle with the tick jobs stubbed to three _session_awaiting reads each; every count is derived in its case from the world's sizes, and what each class pins is in the module's docstring: BoundPerCycleAndPerPass (one validation per cycle and per pass however many readers, backed by a census by call class, and the miss path inside the bound), MissPathRoads (the miss path's roads beside the bound), PerCycleNotSticky (a change between cycles is seen by the next cycle and not the same one), TwoThreadsEachValidateOnce, Guards (each guard against the input it refuses and the one it accepts, an eviction landing inside a read, the two pop paths and the held-root lag), ScopedInvalidation (an eviction drops what depends on its root and nothing else, the fold's root at both edges, the table at its cap), EvictionTableLock (the table's three windows with real threads, and the stale serve end to end), DependencyKey (the key a chat build records for the trees the miss walk read, on every road that answers the lookup, a path one build reported under two keys, and the residual, witnessed), SumOverRoots, and SpyRoads (the census's own roads). Red under the fix reverted and under each named broken variant (sticky scope, module-level memo, no eviction record from the forget or from either pop, the root checked on disk before the hold is served, a held fault, a held unclean walk, a launch fold's fault re-folded per owner lookup or held for the cycle, the fault producer deleted, a stamp or a launch fold served without its root's vouch, a table clear that records no generation, a dependency note from a fresh stat, a note of the root alone, own stats keyed per agent and directory).
