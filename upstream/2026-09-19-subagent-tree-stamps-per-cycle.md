The agent-file lookup's memo (_subagent_file) re-validated a hit by stat-ing every directory its original walk read, which for a nested or missing agent's file is the whole subagents tree; _awaiting_nest resolves every awaiting agent's file per _session_awaiting call, and the pusher makes up to five such calls per session per cycle, the jobs pass one more. So one call over A nested agents paid (A+1) x D directory stats with nothing changed (two or more agents, or a command row whose owner is read from their transcripts), and per pusher cycle or jobs pass, N reads per session, the two loops' own reads paid N x sum over the sessions read of (A_s + 1) x D_s directory stats and N x sum_s A_s launch folds: linear in the reads, the sessions, the agents and the directories at once (round 1 of #882 asked for the cost in this form, derived and with the lab below as its evidence, in place of a per-call figure and a per-root reading of two production totals, which no mechanism here predicts and two kernels cannot separate). The motivation, a dated reading: measured on two deployed kernels on 2026-09-19, a 20 s py-spy profile of one had _dir_stamp's one os.stat as the top self frame, 28 percent of the samples by the profile's reading, over trees of 305, 383 and 72 directories; the other's memos.subagentTree counters showed 24.5 million validation lstats and 22.7 minutes in validateMs over 6.8 hours across 1,294 directories.

The fix keys the validation on the event a time window would have stood in for: the pusher cycle and the jobs pass. Each opens a thread-confined scope on _live_scope (_subagent_scope_open inside the try beside the other per-cycle slots, closed in the finally) holding three memos: the validated (directories, stats) pair per root, each directory's stamp in the shape _dir_stamp answers, and _awaiting_nest's per-agent launch ids keyed (transcript, agentId). The first reader of a tree in the cycle validates or walks it; every later reader on that thread in the cycle, the agent-file lookup's stamp re-check included, pays no stat. Each scope entry carries the root it depends on (the tree's own root for its pair and the stamps indexed from it, the transcript's own subagents root for a launch fold, named from the transcript whatever tree the file resolved under: the fold's root, its bound and the test that executes both are stated once, in _subagent_scope's docstring) and the _SUBAGENT_TREES_GEN value read before its disk read; a root leaving _SUBAGENT_TREES (the forget, per evicted root; the missing-root and replaced-root pops when they removed an entry) moves the gen by one and records the new value against that root in _SUBAGENT_ROOT_EVICTED, and an entry is served only while no eviction of its root has happened since, so an eviction is honoured on both threads for that root's pair, stamps and launch folds and nothing else moves (since 2026-09-21; until then one process-wide value emptied every scope's three maps on any root's eviction, which re-validated every held tree and re-folded every awaiting agent's file on both threads, once per forget rather than once per cycle). The two pops run only on a read the scope does not serve: a root held earlier in the cycle is served until its scope ends (the stale window below), its removal or replacement found by the next cycle's first read on that thread, or by a read with no hold on it (the other loop's first read of it in its own cycle, a handler thread's), whose pop records the eviction so that the holder's next lookup drops the pair. A stamp _dir_stamp took itself, whose tree the scope cannot name (the project directory on an agent-file miss), is vouched by no root and re-taken after any eviction. The table is bounded at 1024 roots; an eviction of a root not in a full table clears it and records the generation, which drops every held entry once. Nothing failed is held: a missing or replaced root, a walk with a failed listing or child lstat, a stamp whose stat raises and a launch fold that did not read the file are answered as before and leave no entry in the scope, and a root whose lstat fails for a reason other than absence (EACCES from a parent, EIO) is neither absent nor held: where the branch took every OSError for absence (it popped the entry, recorded an eviction and noted the tree absent), its readers now answer their standing entries unheld or an unreadable marker and the chat build is told to read again, the shape stated once in _subagent_tree's docstring and executed at both edges by tests/test_subagent_tree_memo.py UnreadableRoot (an EIO by mock, a real EACCES); the faulted fold is held for the one _session_awaiting call that observed it (each of the A agents' owner lookups consults every other agent's launches, so a fault re-folded per lookup cost A x (A - 1) folds per call where a call-local memo costs A) and is folded again by the next call, so a file that became readable is seen then. _dir_stamp now counts each successful os.stat into dirStats, so the counter reads both validators (the lstat half alone before), and a figure from before the deploy and one from after are not one series. The key a chat build records for a subagents tree the agent-file miss walk looked through (_subagent_file_walk) is the served read's (mtime, size) per directory (_subagent_tree_dep_note, the shape the sidecar map already recorded for the own tree), never a stat taken after it: round 1 of #882 found the walk recording each sibling root under a fresh stat taken after the served listing, so a file landing after the hold under a directory the listing lacked was recorded under its own post-landing key, equal to every later re-stat, and the tab that showed the agent's file missing was never rebuilt; and it recorded the root alone, which a landing under a listed child never moves.

Bound per cycle and per pass, for the two loops' own reads: one derived expression in the reads, the sessions, the agents and the directories (each root's one validation, whatever the readers, agents and reads consult it; no stamp stat when each tree is read before its agent-file lookups; one launch fold per agent whose launches are consulted; the miss path's stats once per cycle, shared by every agent whose file is nowhere or under a sibling's tree, however many such agents there are, with the cold walk's own cost per such agent per walk, its realpath lstat of each sibling root included; an eviction's cost confined to the evicted root's re-read and the launch folds keyed on it, the transcript's own subagents root as the paragraph above says; a faulting fold re-folded per read), stated once in _subagent_tree_memo_report's docstring with the lab cells that are its evidence (a lab lifted from the module's world, R sessions of D directories, A agents and N reads, G agents whose file is nowhere and dead sibling trees, through the real _pusher_cycle and _jobs_cycle, run outside the repo with its records kept outside it) and executed in the tree by tests/test_subagent_tree_stamps_per_cycle.py's BoundPerCycleAndPerPass, SumOverRoots, ScopedInvalidation and Guards cases through the real cycles; every other home points there. Round 1 of #882's extra9-3 and fresh-4 asked for the cost in that form (it was stated per call and per tree, with two production totals as its evidence, and the miss path's sibling term as one stat per sibling on a flat sibling tree where the walk pays one per directory); the owner's pass before round 2 found the miss-path term then stated per agent wherever it was stated, in the tree and in the PR body, G such agents predicting G project-directory stats where the code pays 1 (an own stat keyed by directory in the scope), corrected it in the one home and pinned the sharing by a two-agent case. Stale window: a change on disk after the cycle's validation is seen by the next cycle's first reader, one cycle later at most (the pusher's wake or its 0.5 s backstop under the 1 s minimum interval, the jobs thread's 0.5 s pace, plus the cycle's own duration), the tree's own removal or replacement included (the pops run on a read the scope does not serve). Handler-thread builds (WS and HTTP), connect pushes and the act-now nudge pass hold no scope and read per call as before.
