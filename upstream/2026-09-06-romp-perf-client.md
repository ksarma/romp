Two changes beyond the bundles, both stated in the docs: the kernel rotates `client-diag.jsonl` past 8 MB (the rows are about 1 KB per pane per active minute and nothing pruned the file), and the webview RELEASE build keeps identifiers (whitespace and syntax still minified; measured +27% raw and +13.5% gzipped over all webview bundles, `feed.js` 119 KB to 134 KB gzipped) because the browser's long-frame attribution names callbacks by their compile-time name, so a mangled build reported `feed.js:Qe` and a different letter after every rebuild. The browser side goes live on a reload through the per-request dist token; the rotation takes effect at the next kernel restart. A page without `performance.now` gets no telemetry and an unwrapped handler, so the node stand-in tests are untouched. Pairs with the kernel counters row above: that one says what the kernel spent, this one what the browser spent on the frames it received. Not yet offered.
