Four browser-side costs upstream shares. P2: about 58% of all handler time in the recorded live minutes went to panes the shell had hidden with display:none (the timeline band when toggled off, every pane but the current tab on the phone), each rendering a board nobody could see; the per-host state keeps updating underneath so deltas apply and nothing asks for a full frame, hidden is the shell's word (`{romp:'panes', on}`) because a same-size re-show fires no resize, and the chat and feed panes are not held. P4: applyDelta rebuilt every lane's turns array on every bars delta, so a pane had nothing to key a per-lane cache on; touched lanes are the lane prefix of each set/del key plus the lanes whose key subsequence differs when the frame carries an order, and the assembled value is unchanged. P5: the rAF loop redrew the svg every time the live edge moved 0.15 px (twice a second at a one-hour window, up to sixteen times a second at ten minutes); the tick now writes a translate plus a width per rider, 0.046 ms against 7.68 ms a full draw headless at 32 lanes; a glyph riding the live edge or a drift that reaches the gutter gap still takes the full draw, and a lane aging out, a gridline or clock tick entering the window and time-latched visuals wait for the next full draw, bounded by the kernel's 5 s skeleton rebuild. P6: `hljs.highlightAuto` over ten grammars cost about 4 ms a fence on every tail re-render and window rebuild (0.002 ms cached); `renderTabs` rebuilt every tab node and read every offsetTop on each kernel push; the tail re-rendered 25 trailing events on every tail in case an earlier one had changed in place, and the signals that window stood in for are now explicit (a rewind marks the view stale when the prefix's editable set or overlay changed, a full frame rebuilds the window, the worked footer on a turn's last reply is patched by unit).

Stacked on Browser P1 (`federation-direct-frame-delivery`, #265). Port notes: the `perf-telemetry.ts` hunk goes with `romp-perf-client` (fork #219); `worked-footer.ts` is absent from `upstream/main` (2026-09-07), so P6's footer patching needs the fork's footer module or upstream's equivalent; the default-hidden Outline and Waiting panes named in the hold are the fork's layout, and the Waiting pane is fork-only. Review found one blocker (the footer plan could not reach a pre-`from` reply the same tail demoted or completed) and four should-fix items, all fixed and pinned.
