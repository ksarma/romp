Upstream ships the walk this pins: the wake-only mode is its #936, the parse gate its T401 and the shared decision read its #1141, and jobs stage 1 (fork PR 784) made the wake-only look record and skip on the ten-file key. Fold ruling A condition 7 required exactly one shared load per alive session with the toggle off; stage 1's first wording made that at most one and dropped the floor. The reviewer's ruling of 2026-09-19 restored the floor and then restated the condition per mechanism, since a single call count is falsified by any new legitimate reader where a named mechanism adds a clause: the condition bounds two loaders of a session's goal store on the walk's road, the look's decision read and the placement gate's currency check, each on its own. The walk takes at most one shared goal-store load per alive session per pass, exactly one when its look reaches the store and zero when the look is skipped (a skipped look repeats its verdict and writes nothing, so it needs no data) or ends at a state gate before the store read; the placement gate's post-derivation currency check is a separate load, at most one per derived session, counted apart. Other readers of the same store run on the same pass under their own rules and outside both bounds; the reference's jobs block names them with their bounds and their callers on the toggle-off pass. The ruling asked for a test that counts loads per pass against whether the look ran or skipped, red in both directions, with a counter added if none exists. None did: memos.nudgeWalk counted looks, parses and skips but not the store reads, and memos.shared counts every reader's calls on the shared cache, not the walk's. So this adds memos.nudgeWalk.loads, bumped at the walk's one call site (the gate's check is the gate's own and is not counted there), names it in the _PerfStats gloss and the reference's memos.nudgeWalk entry, states both bounds in the reference's jobs block, and pins them in tests/test_nudge_walk_one_load_per_pass.py: the real pass over two alive sessions with real transcript files and stores; a recorder on km.jd.load_goals_shared, the inner shared door both of the judge's boundary wrappers reach (load_goals_shared_or_fault resolves the name from the judge's globals at each call and hands the object to _or_fault, which resolves no door name itself), that records each call's caller by function, file and line, stepping over the judge's boundary frames by code identity, and calls through; a second recorder on km.jd.load_goals, the writer's door, asserted empty after every pass with the same naming; the wake sweep (_awaiting_wake_outcomes) named as the store's third reader and held to one shared load per wake record it owns per pass; a per-pass reconciliation of the shared cache's call counters (hit, miss, compare_miss, absent, fallback) against the recorded calls, so a load through a door no recorder wraps is noticed; a source census over the look's own source and over the real body of every helper the fixture replaces, and a census of the walk's counter across kernel/kernel.py (every reference by name classified; no other identifier or string constant spelling its name there or in kernel/judge.py) that holds its one write of loads to the look's bump, the statement directly before the read, which is a statement of the look's own body; the walk's and the gate's counts asserted separately per pass and per session and never summed, the gate's as the bound (at most one check per derive) with equality only where the fixture caches every parse; the served counter beside the walk's count. Per session: the walk loads once on the first pass and on a ledger-driven run pass, zero on the skip passes, one beside zero when one session's transcript moves, zero on a look a state gate ends first, and one, counted, on a look whose store read faults; the gate checks once per derived session and never when served or skipped; the sweep reads once per owned record on every pass, with a store whose nodes lack the goal and with no store file; no writer load. Tier fix because a served counter is added. Depends on the perf-export-public entry (fork #764) for the export-fold assertion, since the module loads cli/perf_public.py, which that entry adds, at import; and on jobs stage 1 (fork #784) for the wake-only record and skip, which the kernel hunk's context carries. Mutations, each landed and reverted (the figures are in the module docstring): a read before the memo is consulted reds the first pass on the walk's ceiling, two per session, and the skip's early return dropped reds the skip pass on the walk's bound; the load gone from the run path (a stale per-sid snapshot reused) reds the run pass on the walk's bound; a load through the inner door planted in the pass loop, at the tick's setup, inside the look or inside the sweep reds named by function, file and line or on the sweep's bound; a load through the writer door or its boundary wrapper at the same sites is named the same way, for its kernel caller and never for the judge's _or_fault; a call through a reference to the real door taken before the recorder stood is caught by the counter reconciliation; a load inside a replaced helper's real body is caught by the source census.
