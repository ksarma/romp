Upstream ships the walk this pins: the wake-only mode is its #936, the parse gate its T401 and the shared decision read its #1141, and jobs stage 1 (fork PR 784) made the wake-only look record and skip on the ten-file key. Fold ruling A condition 7 required exactly one shared load per alive session with the toggle off; stage 1's first wording made that at most one and dropped the floor. The reviewer's ruling of 2026-09-19 restored the floor and then restated the condition per mechanism, since a single call count is falsified by any new legitimate reader where a named mechanism adds a clause: the condition bounds two loaders of a session's goal store on the walk's road, the look's decision read and the placement gate's currency check, each on its own. The walk takes at most one shared goal-store load per alive session per pass, exactly one when its look reaches the store and zero when the look is skipped (a skipped look repeats its verdict and writes nothing, so it needs no data) or ends at a state gate before the store read; the placement gate's post-derivation currency check is a separate load, at most one per derived session, counted apart. Other readers of the same store run on the same pass under their own rules and outside both bounds; the reference's jobs block names them with their bounds and their callers on the toggle-off pass. The ruling asked for a test that counts loads per pass against whether the look ran or skipped, red in both directions, with a counter added if none exists. None did: memos.nudgeWalk counted looks, parses and skips but not the store reads, and memos.shared counts every reader's calls on the shared cache, not the walk's.
