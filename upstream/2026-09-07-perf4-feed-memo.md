Two pusher-thread costs upstream shares. P1: the feed's per-session store read took the writer's loader on every build outside a judge pass, re-parsing stores the timeline and the chat already held in jd.load_goals_shared (the pusher's single largest raw_decode caller); the live branch now reads the shared view, and _feed_goals_view returns the served store with a key that stands for its content (the object itself where its identity implies its content, a never-equal sentinel where it does not: a rewind hold, a failed gesture replay, the cache off, no store file). The landing gate builds the feed over a world that exercises every store read and asserts the shared cache stayed on with no poisoned write; a source pin refuses an assignment into the store on the read path. P2-A: the per-session loop recomputed values that are pure functions of the parse and the store (the seam maps, the tree shape, each top's flattened tree) on every build; they are memoized per session on the parse object, the store's identity key, the names registry by value, the working bit, the session row's name and colour, and the remembered anchors a cold row read, with every bypass counted and never stored. Rows became shared values, so the serving fold re-parents onto a copy and the age tint moves from the card builders to serialization. Offline bench on the sealed state copy (31 sessions): build_feed 603 to 170 ms median (P1 alone 508), noparse 335 to 117; a replay at the profile's change rates served 96.7% of sessions from the memo.
