bin/romp-manager grants each kernel SHUTDOWN_GRACE_MS after its SIGTERM before the SIGKILL (8000 ms since the grace was raised from 5 s; ROMP_SHUTDOWN_GRACE_MS overrides it) and exits at that grace plus 200 ms at the latest, but `romp down` polled for it 28 times a quarter second (7 s nominal), so a kernel that used its exit budget made the down report a manager that would not stop, exit 1 and take the down marker back. The fork sizes the poll from the grace plus a 2 s margin, and gives the grace ONE authoritative source and ONE contract (pull-in review round 2, 2026-09-16): the manager's GET /status carries manager.shutdownGraceMs, the value it runs with, and `romp down` sizes its poll from that field of the status answer it already fetches, falling back to ROMP_SHUTDOWN_GRACE_MS in its own shell and a 10 s floor only when the field is absent (a manager started by a bare `romp up` or `--foreground` reads the knob from the shell that launched it, which the down's shell need not share); both readers take a plain whole number of milliseconds, one to nine digits, above zero, and any other form (an exponent, hex, a sign, a fraction, whitespace, a leading zero) is the 8000 default, where the manager's `Number(x) || 8000` took 2e4 and 0x2ee0 and the CLI's read did not (shutdownGrace in bin/romp-manager, exported for tests/manager-down.test.js; nine digits also keeps the value under setTimeout's bound). The kernel-drain poll on the down's direct-SIGTERM road (a bare kernel, or one that outlived its manager) reuses that bound: it was a fixed 24 x 0.25 s under a comment calling the drain about 2 s, while a kernel's exit budgets are shares of the grace (7.5 s of an 8 s grace), so a kernel draining on its parent-death watchdog's thread, its accept loop still answering, outran it. The stale 5 s comments beside the manager's SHUTDOWN_GRACE_MS and beside the poll, the bin/README.md row, and kernel/kernel.py's two exit-budget comments (beside EXIT_DRAIN_BUDGET_S and in _drain_and_exit, upstream's own lines: the budgets are shares of the grace the kernel was spawned with, the manager's 8 s by default, a bare kernel's own 5 s) are corrected. Pinned in tests/romp.bats with a real manager under a 12 s grace and a kernel that holds its SIGTERM for 11.5 s; with the knob on the manager's launch line alone (20 s, unset in the down's shell) and a 19.5 s hold; and with a fake kernel holding its SIGTERM for 7.5 s on a background thread; and in tests/manager-down.test.js for the rejected forms falling to 8000 and the field on /status. Upstream bin/romp at 14f1548a9 carries the same loops and comments, and its bin/romp-manager the Number() read. Found by the pull-in review, 2026-09-16.
