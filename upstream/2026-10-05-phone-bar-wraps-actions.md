On a phone the bottom bar's action buttons ran past the right edge of the screen, out of reach. The bar is one flex row: the pane tabs (flex:1, never narrower than their labels, 8px apart), a divider and six buttons that never shrink (Usage, Remote kernels, Restart, the Log's triangle, the push bell, Settings; 28 to 32px each), and the push script reveals the bell on every page. With the fork's default tabs the row needed 418px in Chromium and 413px in WebKit and Firefox, and the bar does not scroll: at 390px Settings was past the edge, at 375 the bell partly past it and from 360 out of reach, at 320 the Log's triangle as well. Two changes. Usage, Remote kernels and Restart kernel moved into the settings card: the first two are the bar's panels about the machinery rather than the sessions, and the kernel's restart is machinery too, rarely used and costly to hit by accident; the bell, whose state reads at a glance, stays on the bar. In the card they are a row of three buttons under the title, shown on the phone layout only and dressed as the card's other word buttons (Customize shortcuts' rules, not the menu vocabulary of a dropdown's rows). Each closes the card and runs the handler its bar button ran, through a message the shell accepts for those three acts from the settings document alone, and the Remote kernels glyph there is still painted by the shell's poll; the card's opening, a word the shell reads from the settings frame's window alone, brings that copy in step with the rail's glyph (the icon's live classes, the last poll's node colours) and paints nothing on the rail, so an Attach in flight keeps its busy there. The row shows only where the shell publishes a marker beside the listener that runs the three (__rompPhoneActs), so a phone shell still running the build before the move, with the three on its bar, gets no row whose taps would run nothing; and an open card follows the shell's layout word (the re-tell on its layout query's change), so the row hides and shows as the window crosses the phone breakpoint; a card the Token usage panel hides follows it too, so the panel's close shows the row the layout calls for. A drop's flash shows at the drop or not at all: its class, left on a copy that runs no animation, is cleared when the card shows again, at its opening and at the Token usage panel's close. The accent marks that glyph alone, as on the rail, and the button's label keeps the card's text colour. The glyph sits on its button's own fill, which the button keeps on hover, where the shell's dialing grey and needs-you red (#8a8a8a and #e5484d) read 2.59 and 2.94:1 in the light theme, so in that theme the card's glyph wears darker ones, #777777 and #db3d5a, through two feed.css tokens; the red is turned in hue as well, from 23 to 15 degrees in OKLCH, so it sits 9.04 from the connected node's clay (#c2410c) in OKLab distance x100, no closer than the shell's pair (8.94). Every colour the glyph wears reads at 3:1 or more on the card and on that fill, at rest and hovered, in both themes. With no usage reading yet, Usage is shown disabled with the line "No reading yet" rather than closing the card to open nothing, and the row's handler returns on a disabled button, since a script's click on one still reaches it; where the shell's newest read of the readings that has ended failed (an error status, no answer, or none within the 10 s bound the card's pull carries), Usage is disabled with the line "Couldn't load" instead, never "No reading yet". A read that ends after a later read has ended changes neither that state nor, with an error status, the readings, so a reopened card's earlier pull, failing late, cannot turn the later pull's answer into "Couldn't load". Usage keeps one box in every state, the loader's included, so the row and the tabs under it do not move when the pull ends. Each opening asks the shell for a fresh pull (__rompUsagePull, the usage script's own fetch of its readings, the one the panel's opener makes), Usage showing the romp loader and taking no tap until it ends, then reads __rompUsageReading, the usage script's own test over its readings (the check the panel's opener makes); the shell's renderRows, the one writer of those readings, tells an open card whenever they change, the card under the Token usage panel included, so the line never outlives them. A shell that cannot be asked leaves Usage as its bar button was, enabled with no line. The row left needs 322px in Chromium and 318px in WebKit and Firefox, and 358 and 353px with the Files tab on, so the bar is one row at every phone width from 360px with the Files tab on or off. Where one row still does not fit (320px in Chromium with the default tabs, 320px in all three engines with the Files tab on), the divider and the actions, now one element (.mtabs-acts), wrap together to a second row at the right edge, and a ResizeObserver on the bar re-runs barfit() so the strip the panes leave follows a wrap that comes with no resize. The desktop rail is unchanged, and the card shows no row there. A served test measures the bar at six phone widths in portrait and landscape, with and without the Files tab, clicks each moved action from the bar's Settings, reads the card's glyph lit, marching while a host attaches and unlit with no host up, reads Usage with and without a reading, with a reading landing in the open card, with the readings emptying and filling under the Token usage panel, over a reading the shell has not pulled (the loader up while the opening's pull is held), over an error status, a pull failed in transit and a pull never answered, each followed by an ok answer with no rows, and over a reopened card's earlier pull ending after the later one, dispatches a script's clicks on a disabled Usage, measures the row's height in each of Usage's states, opens the card on a shell without the marker, posts the layout word from windows other than the shell's, follows the row across 820px with the card open and with the panel over it, measures the glyph's colours against the card and the button's fill, at rest and hovered, and the red's distance from the connected colour, in both themes, and reads the desktop rail and the card opened from it, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox; it is red at the wrap-only head.
