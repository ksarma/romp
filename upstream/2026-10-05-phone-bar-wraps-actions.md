On a phone the bottom bar's action buttons ran past the right edge of the screen, out of reach. The bar is one flex row: the pane tabs (flex:1, never narrower than their labels, 8px apart), a divider and six buttons that never shrink (Usage, Remote kernels, Restart, the Log's triangle, the push bell, Settings; 28 to 32px each). The push script reveals the bell on every page, so it is always there. With the fork's default tabs the row needs 418px in Chromium and 413px in WebKit and Firefox, and the bar does not scroll: at 390px Settings was past the edge, at 360 the bell too, at 320 Restart was cut and the Log's triangle, the bell and Settings were past it, so the Log could not be opened from its triangle. The divider and the actions are now one element (.mtabs-acts) and the bar may wrap: when the row does not fit, the actions move together to a second row at the right edge and the tabs fill the first at the single row's height (a min-height); where the row fits, the tabs do not move, and the actions do not move in Chromium or Firefox and move by at most 0.03px in WebKit. A ResizeObserver on the bar re-runs barfit(), so the strip the panes leave follows a wrap that comes with no resize (the Files tab turned on, the webfont arriving). A served test measures every control at six phone widths in portrait and landscape, with and without the Files tab, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox; it is red before the change at every width whose row overflows, in all three.

upstream/main (last updated 2026-09-24) has the same bar: its kernel/kernel.py carries every #mtabs rule above byte for byte, the same six actions, the bell revealed on every page by the same line of its push script, and the same barfit and refit bindings (without the fork's later rebinding of barfit). Its phone tabs come from its pane records: Chat, Sessions, Outline and Feed by default, Files when its control is on (its experimental Artifacts pane has no phone tab). By the widths measured here for those labels and buttons its default row needs 367px in Chromium and 361 to 362px in WebKit and Firefox, so it overflows the same way at 360 and 320px, and with the Files tab on (403px, and 396 to 397px) at 375 and 390px too (derived, not run against that tree). The kernel change applies there as it is: the observer line goes beside the same refit bindings and calls barfit by name. The served test counts five default tabs and six with the Files tab on, which an offer changes to upstream's four and five, and it takes its port and readiness wait from tests/lab_ports.py and its bundles from tests/lab_dist.py, which upstream does not have yet (upstream/2026-10-02-tests-served-lab-ports.md, upstream/2026-09-09-lab-dist-copy-race.md), so an offer carries those calls in upstream's own form.
