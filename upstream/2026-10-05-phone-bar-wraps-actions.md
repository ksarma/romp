On a phone the bottom bar's action buttons ran past the right edge of the screen, out of reach. The bar is one flex row: the pane tabs (flex:1, never narrower than their labels, 8px apart), a divider and six buttons that never shrink (Usage, Remote kernels, Restart, the Log's triangle, the push bell, Settings; 28 to 32px each), and the push script reveals the bell on every page. With the fork's default tabs the row needed 418px in Chromium and 413px in WebKit and Firefox, and the bar does not scroll: at 390px Settings was past the edge, at 360 the bell too, at 320 the Log's triangle as well. Two changes. Usage and Remote kernels, the bar's two panels about the machinery rather than the sessions, moved into the settings card: a row of two buttons under the card's title, shown on the phone layout, each closing the card and running the handler its bar button ran, and the Remote kernels glyph there still painted by the shell's poll. The row left needs 354px in Chromium and 349 to 350px in WebKit and Firefox (moving either one alone left 386px in Chromium), so the bar is one row from 375 to 430px. Where one row still does not fit (320px, up to 353px in Chromium and 349px in WebKit and Firefox, and below 390 or 385px with the Files tab on), the divider and the actions, now one element (.mtabs-acts), wrap together to a second row at the right edge, and a ResizeObserver on the bar re-runs barfit() so the strip the panes leave follows a wrap that comes with no resize. The desktop rail is unchanged. A served test measures the bar at six phone widths in portrait and landscape, with and without the Files tab, clicks each moved action from the bar's Settings and reads the desktop rail, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox; it is red at the wrap-only head.

upstream/main (last updated 2026-09-24) has the same bar and the same card: its kernel/kernel.py carries the same six actions, the same #mtabs rules the change sits beside, the bell revealed on every page by the same line of its push script, the same barfit and refit bindings (without the fork's later rebinding of barfit), the same A-map, mnet and paintIcon, the gear in the same #f-settings page and the same __rompMobileOn, and its ui/webview/gear.js the same card title and the same Open log route the new buttons copy. Its phone tabs come from its pane records: Chat, Sessions, Outline and Feed by default, Files when its control is on (its experimental Artifacts pane has no phone tab). By the widths measured here for those labels and buttons its default row needed 367px in Chromium and 361 to 362px in WebKit and Firefox, and with the two actions moved needs 303px and 297 to 298px, one row at every phone width from 320px; with the Files tab on, 339px and 332 to 333px (derived, not run against that tree). The change applies there as it is. The served test counts five default tabs and six with the Files tab on, which an offer changes to upstream's four and five, and it takes its port and readiness wait from tests/lab_ports.py and its bundles from tests/lab_dist.py, which upstream does not have yet (upstream/2026-10-02-tests-served-lab-ports.md, upstream/2026-09-09-lab-dist-copy-race.md), so an offer carries those calls in upstream's own form.
