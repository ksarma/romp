On a phone the bottom bar's action buttons ran past the right edge of the screen, out of reach. The bar is one flex row: the pane tabs (flex:1, never narrower than their labels, 8px apart), a divider and six buttons that never shrink (Usage, Remote kernels, Restart, the Log's triangle, the push bell, Settings; 28 to 32px each), and the push script reveals the bell on every page. With the fork's default tabs the row needed 418px in Chromium and 413px in WebKit and Firefox, and the bar does not scroll: at 390px Settings was past the edge, at 360 the bell too, at 320 the Log's triangle as well. Two changes. Usage, Remote kernels and Restart kernel moved into the settings card: the first two are the bar's panels about the machinery rather than the sessions, and the kernel's restart is machinery too, rarely used and costly to hit by accident; the bell, whose state reads at a glance, stays on the bar. In the card they are a row of three buttons under the title, shown on the phone layout only and dressed as the card's other word buttons (Customize shortcuts' rules, not the menu vocabulary of a dropdown's rows). Each closes the card and runs the handler its bar button ran, through a message the shell accepts for those three acts from the settings document alone, and the Remote kernels glyph there is still painted by the shell's poll. That glyph sits on its button's own fill, where the shell's dialing grey and needs-you red (#8a8a8a and #e5484d) read 2.59 and 2.94:1 in the light theme, so in that theme the card's glyph wears darker ones, #777777 and #dc3f46, through two feed.css tokens; every colour it wears reads at 3:1 or more on the card and on that fill in both themes. With no usage reading yet, Usage is shown disabled with the line "No reading yet" rather than closing the card to open nothing; the card asks the shell at each opening, through __rompUsageReading, the usage script's own test over its readings (the check the panel's opener makes). The row left needs 322px in Chromium and 318px in WebKit and Firefox, and 358 and 353px with the Files tab on, so the bar is one row at every phone width from 360px with the Files tab on or off. Where one row still does not fit (320px in Chromium with the default tabs, 320px in all three engines with the Files tab on), the divider and the actions, now one element (.mtabs-acts), wrap together to a second row at the right edge, and a ResizeObserver on the bar re-runs barfit() so the strip the panes leave follows a wrap that comes with no resize. The desktop rail is unchanged, and the card shows no row there. A served test measures the bar at six phone widths in portrait and landscape, with and without the Files tab, clicks each moved action from the bar's Settings, reads Usage with and without a reading, measures the glyph's colours against the card and the button's fill in both themes, and reads the desktop rail and the card opened from it, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox; it is red at the wrap-only head.

upstream/main (last updated 2026-09-24) has the same bar and the same card: its kernel/kernel.py carries the same six actions, the same #mtabs rules the change sits beside, the bell revealed on every page by the same line of its push script, the same barfit and refit bindings (without the fork's later rebinding of barfit), the same A-map with the same restart handler, mnet and paintIcon, the usage script's __rompUsagePanel and openIt (its tipHTML also names the attached machines with no usage report yet, so there the reading test, being openIt's own, is true whenever the panel has that line to show), the gear in the same #f-settings page and the same __rompMobileOn, and its ui/webview/gear.js and gear.css the same card title, the same Open log route the new buttons copy and the same Customize shortcuts button whose dress the row copies (its button-vocab.test.ts counts the same three families), and its feed.css the same light theme (the card #FAF5EE, the button fill #E7DED2). Its phone tabs come from its pane records: Chat, Sessions, Outline and Feed by default, Files when its control is on (its experimental Artifacts pane has no phone tab). By the widths measured here for those labels and buttons (the restart button 32px in all three engines) its default row needed 367px in Chromium and 361 to 362px in WebKit and Firefox, and with the three actions moved needs 271px and 265 to 266px, one row at every phone width from 320px; with the Files tab on, 307px and 300 to 301px (derived, not run against that tree). The change does not apply there as it is: upstream's shell checks the source of every message it acts on (window.__rompPaneSourceOk, fail-closed), and its tests/test_pane_registry.py requires every shell listener to read that check as its first statement. An offer carries three changes for that: the two new message listeners, the phoneAct listener in _LANDING_MOBILE_JS and the repaint when the settings card opens in _LANDING_REMOTES_JS, read window.__rompPaneSourceOk first; tests/fixtures/landing-code-panes.json is regenerated, because the new #mtabs button[data-pane]{min-height:30px} rule falls in the slice of the shell's style rules that its code-panes test compares (column_css); and the served test posts its usage reading from a pane frame, because upstream's usage listener drops a message whose source is the shell's own window. The served test counts five default tabs and six with the Files tab on, which an offer changes to upstream's four and five, and it takes its port and readiness wait from tests/lab_ports.py and its bundles from tests/lab_dist.py, which upstream does not have yet (upstream/2026-10-02-tests-served-lab-ports.md, upstream/2026-09-09-lab-dist-copy-race.md), so an offer carries those calls in upstream's own form.
