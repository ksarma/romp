On a phone the bottom bar's action buttons ran past the right edge of the screen, out of reach. The bar is one flex row: the pane tabs (flex:1, never narrower than their labels, 8px apart), a divider and six buttons that never shrink (Usage, Remote kernels, Restart, the Log's triangle, the push bell, Settings; 28 to 32px each), and the push script reveals the bell on every page. With the fork's default tabs the row needed 418px in Chromium and 413px in WebKit and Firefox, and the bar does not scroll: at 390px Settings was past the edge, at 375 the bell partly past it and from 360 out of reach, at 320 the Log's triangle as well. Two changes. Usage, Remote kernels and Restart kernel moved into the settings card: the first two are the bar's panels about the machinery rather than the sessions, and the kernel's restart is machinery too, rarely used and costly to hit by accident; the bell, whose state reads at a glance, stays on the bar. In the card they are a row of three buttons under the title, shown on the phone layout only and dressed as the card's other word buttons (Customize shortcuts' rules, not the menu vocabulary of a dropdown's rows). Each closes the card and runs the handler its bar button ran, through a message the shell accepts for those three acts from the settings document alone, and the Remote kernels glyph there is still painted by the shell's poll; the card's opening, a word the shell reads from the settings frame's window alone, brings that copy in step with the rail's glyph (the icon's live classes, the last poll's node colours) and paints nothing on the rail, so an Attach in flight keeps its busy there. The row shows only where the shell publishes a marker beside the listener that runs the three (__rompPhoneActs), so a phone shell still running the build before the move, with the three on its bar, gets no row whose taps would run nothing; and an open card follows the shell's layout word (the re-tell on its layout query's change), so the row hides and shows as the window crosses the phone breakpoint; a card the Token usage panel hides follows it too, so the panel's close shows the row the layout calls for. A drop's flash shows at the drop or not at all: its class, left on a copy that runs no animation, is cleared when the card shows again, at its opening and at the Token usage panel's close. The accent marks that glyph alone, as on the rail, and the button's label keeps the card's text colour. The glyph sits on its button's own fill, which the button keeps on hover, where the shell's dialing grey and needs-you red (#8a8a8a and #e5484d) read 2.59 and 2.94:1 in the light theme, so in that theme the card's glyph wears darker ones, #777777 and #db3d5a, through two feed.css tokens; the red is turned in hue as well, from 23 to 15 degrees in OKLCH, so it sits 9.04 from the connected node's clay (#c2410c) in OKLab distance x100, no closer than the shell's pair (8.94). Every colour the glyph wears reads at 3:1 or more on the card and on that fill, at rest and hovered, in both themes, and the unlit glyph keeps its own colour on hover, so hover never makes it look lit. Usage keeps its resting fill on hover too: on the row's accent wash its muted "Couldn't load" line read 4.38:1 in the dark theme, under the 4.5:1 text needs, and on its fill it reads 5.44:1 (5.37:1 in the light theme). Every label in the row keeps its resting colour on hover as well, by one rule for the row's buttons: the accent the labels took there read 3.89:1 on the light theme's button fill under Usage's name and the Remote kernels label, and 4.15:1 on that theme's wash under Restart kernel's, under 4.5:1. A glyph that wears its button's colour still turns accent on hover; Usage's reads 3.89:1 on its fill in the light theme, over the 3:1 a graphic needs. With no usage reading yet, Usage is shown disabled with the line "No reading yet" rather than closing the card to open nothing, and the row's handler returns on a disabled button, since a script's click on one still reaches it; where the shell's newest read of the readings that has ended failed (an error status, no answer, or none within the 10 s bound the card's pull carries), Usage says "Couldn't load" instead, never "No reading yet": disabled where the shell holds no reading, and enabled where it still holds one, whose panel the tap opens at once, its age lines showing how old the reading is, while a refresh runs behind it; the open panel follows the refresh's answer where it brings a reading (an error status empties the readings, which closes the panel, its backdrop with it), and the refresh never opens the panel itself, so a panel closed before it ends stays closed. Every read of the readings goes through one bounded helper: the card's pull, the panel opener's, and the readout's click and 60 s refresh. In an engine without AbortSignal.timeout an AbortController with a timer of the same 10 s aborts the pull; in one whose fetch cannot be aborted (Safari before 12.1: no AbortController before 11.1, and one that fetch ignores in 11.1 and 12.0) the pull runs unbounded. A read that ends after a later read has ended changes neither that state nor, with an error status, the readings, so a reopened card's earlier pull, failing late, cannot turn the later pull's answer into "Couldn't load"; the timeline's forward of a reading takes a place in the same order, so a pull started before it cannot either. Usage is one line tall in every state, as the row's other buttons are, and holds the loader's width, the loader showing in the name's place, so loading to a reading moves neither the row nor the tabs under it, and the row is no taller for Usage; a line saying what the reads found sits beside the name, and a pull that ends in one adds a line to the row only on a narrow window (from 344 to 368px in Chromium and from 341 to 364px in WebKit and Firefox).
