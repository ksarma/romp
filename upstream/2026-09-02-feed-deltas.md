title: Feed deltas, loud socket drops, event-keyed stale banner. Measured 2026-09-02 on a live kernel: every feed push was the whole frame (5.76 MB on a board of about 660 cards; asks 4.8 MB, ledgers 0.95 MB, all else 13 KB), re-sent every few seconds during activity when 0 to 21 cards and 0 to 1 ledgers differed, and the per-card `trgb` colour ticked with the clock so a colour step re-sent everything. Three frames (`WS_QUEUE_BYTES`) got a client dropped by `_mk_ws_send`, silently (`_send_client` swallowed it; the shim logged nothing on close), and each reconnect's 1 s `armStale` timer fired before the resync frame: about 300 stale-banner raises a day, almost all on the feed pane. Three layers. (1) `{type:"feedDelta"}` to a caught-up client that announced `?caps=feedDelta` (upserts by `itemId`/`sid`, removals, `top` whole when changed); every pane page announces `readyGate` on its socket and the kernel holds every push to that socket until the bundle's `ready` (the shim opens the socket before the bundle has loaded; a frame that landed first was lost, and the `ready` frame was then deduped against it); the cached full frame is served on that `ready`, stamped with the time of the serve rather than of the build (the pusher builds only while a client is connected, so a frame cached overnight carried a clock a night old), and the shim re-sends `ready` on a reconnect once the bundle has sent its own (a reconnecting pane waited 7 to 24 s; a redial that completes before the bundle has loaded sends nothing, and a second `ready` on one socket re-serves the frame); the pane anchors its live clock on the frame's `now` paired with its wire arrival (`nowAt`, stamped by federation), so a re-emitted merged frame never moves an age; `needFullFeed` re-base; `trgb` out of deltas and out of the dedup signature (full frames keep it for older bundles), the client computes the tint from `t` on a live clock. (2) One `ws drop:` stderr line and a bell row per drop, logged at the raise site (`_mk_ws_send` → `_note_ws_drop`, so every direct `client["send"]` caller is covered) and naming the push slot whose frame tipped the budget when a push did (the bell shows at most 5 drop rows, none older than an hour), a `wsclose` clientDiag with code, reason and socket age for every socket that opened, and one coalesced `wsconnfail` row for the redials an outage refused. (3) The stale banner raises on the SECOND keepalive arriving before the resync (one heartbeat period with no resync) or on the reconnected socket closing, never on a timer; a reconnect re-sends `ready` once the bundle has: a feed pane is served its cached frame, every other pane its connect push.

Upstream ships the same pusher, shim and feed, so it ports as-is. Wire contract additions only: full `feed` frames still go to every client that does not announce (the VS Code pipes, the Outline pane, remote federation sockets, older bundles) and still carry `trgb`, so an older bundle that destructures it (`const [r,g,b] = it.trgb`) keeps working — a stale tab, or a federated dashboard on a host running the previous build; deltas omit it. The hold is announced too (`readyGate` on the shim's socket URL): a socket that does not announce it is pushed to from accept as before, so federation's remote relays and an older dashboard's sockets are unchanged. Reviewed adversarially 2026-09-03, two rounds. Round one filed 22 items: 14 distinct defects after duplicates, two measurements, one vocabulary flag. Round two confirmed seven distinct items: two majors (the first frame still lost, now to the pusher's own cycle, after the accept-time push was removed; every age anchored on the served frame's hours-old `now`) and five minor ones (an unstamped age, two test gaps, the drop line's lost slot, this row's overstatement). All fixed on the branch; the pusher race was re-run in headless Chromium after the fix (40 loopback loads and 30 with the bundle 350 ms slower, none stuck). Round three confirmed four: two majors (the shim re-sent `ready` on a redial that completed before the bundle had loaded, so the kernel served the frame to a page with no listener and deduped the bundle's own `ready` — 3 of 3 headless loads stuck with the first socket dropped mid-load; the pane re-anchored its live clock on the emit time of every re-emitted merged frame, so a tab drag or an attached kernel's repost moved every age back by the quiet period) and two minor (three ready gates unpinned; a stale tuple comment). All fixed; the reconnect harness re-run after the fix paints 3 of 3, and 20 loopback loads plus 10 with the bundle 350 ms slower stay unstuck.
