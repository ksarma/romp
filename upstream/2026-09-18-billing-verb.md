Until this only the dashboard could change which account a session bills. `romp billing <session> <pick>` posts the same setAuth op the Billing menu posts (a name, a sid or an attached host's session, forwarded as /end forwards), so the session reconnects at its next quiet moment; `--now` cuts the turn in flight after the pick is recorded, so the cut turn's settle arms the reconnect (no new arm path; a compaction is refused, not cut); `default` clears the session's own pick, a value the dashboard has no op for, through the follower walk's per-session move; `--all-following <pick>` writes a pick on every live follower of the machine default through that walk, with no /auth chip; the bare verb prints the launched side, the pick and the machine default, for a dormant session from its reg. The CLI and the kernel routes are upstream's code; the lab test runs the real bin/romp against a hermetic kernel with the fake CLI and skips without the SDK venv. Rebased onto the reviewer's round 1 of the auth-default fix (2026-09-18): the verb's pending writes carry the stored login beside the side, its slot flags are written in the pending's hold, its never-landed branch and `default` dispatch key on the cannot-tell class (a report, or a live host lease with none; a parked pick then waits for the CLI's first init, which decides it), the walk survives one follower's fault, and its words say when a relaunch waits for its spawn slot ("staggered") or for the CLI's first report ("report").
