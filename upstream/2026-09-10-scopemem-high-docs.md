Corrects guidance the parent entry 2026-09-06-session-memory-limits shipped upstream (their #998, commit ac0f1808f: at that commit, upstream docs/reference.md lines 947-956 carry the 16G/12G worked example, and bin/romp-cli-scope lines 61-62 carry the MEMORY_HIGH comment row, which describes a soft limit as throttled and reclaimed, never killed, and recommends no value). Measured 2026-09-10 on the administrator's box with a 12G soft limit under a 16G cap and MemorySwapMax=0: a node process allocating tens of GB of anonymous memory crept toward the cap at about 50 MiB per 5 s while the machine sat at PSI memory-full 42 percent for minutes; with the two equal, the cgroup OOM kill ended it at once inside its own scope. The throttle delay grows with the overage, so the rate is that pair's.
