One rule at every site: a reader that cannot parse a row must not report the store absent and must not take down callers that had nothing to do with the bad row; it skips the row, names the session and the key in the log, and keeps the board. Upstream-facing part: one type-wrong rev, t or expiresAt in one row of one session's notices/<sid>.jsonl raised ValueError out of every feed build (the push cycle and GET /feed.json alike); the shared parse now skips that row and says it once per episode, naming the session, key, field and type, never the text, on stderr and as one bell row per file per episode. Also upstream: the held-mail Approve button's border reads the accent token through color-mix instead of a dark-accent literal, so the light theme's text and edge share a hue. Fork-only (upstream deleted the kernel's quarantine cards; the fork keeps them): a hold's card stands through Clear-all because the reader takes no cleared ledger and _clear_all declines a hold's id at the one write every door reaches, and the directory reader is the bus's _list_json_records shape ported (a record it read and cannot parse moves aside once and is said; a record it cannot read is skipped, said once and left in place for the build that can read it; a directory it cannot list is said once per episode; the board is kept). The bus's own readers of its stores take the same rule, and those apply upstream (upstream keeps the bus's held-mail store, its readers and the GET /quarantine route): a directory the bus cannot list is one bell row per episode for an outbox or readbox with the parked mail left standing, a 503 with the reason from GET /quarantine instead of an empty list, and the listing is os.listdir, never Path.glob, which on Python 3.12 swallowed the PermissionError (the recall's outbox arm included). A held record the bus cannot read or parse, or that is not an object, is skipped and said once in the log with the file left in place for the kernel's reader, a type-wrong `at` sorts as the oldest instead of raising out of the sort, and a decide on a record that is not an object is refused in plain words. The notices reader also reads bytes and skips a line that is not UTF-8 (a whole-file decode raised UnicodeDecodeError out of every build), keeps the first row of a file saved with a BOM, and says a notices directory or file it cannot list, stat or read once per episode instead of drawing a clean board.

The review of 2026-09-20 changed the following, under one statement of the three states a reader of a store meets, of which only the middle one earns a rename: bytes it could not read, bytes it read and could not parse, and a record that parsed whose field has an unexpected type. Fork-only, the kernel's held-mail reader and the clear door: a hold file the kernel cannot read is skipped and left in place for the next build, said once per file and fault on stderr and the bell, and the move aside is reserved for bytes read and not parsed. A hold whose only fault is a body that is not text keeps its card; its gist names the body's type on the kernel's card and in the bus's summary, Deny and an Approve that supplies edited text work on it, and a bare Approve is refused in plain words with the message still held. A hold whose id the bus would refuse to decide never becomes a card: it is moved aside and named by file, never by body. A held-mail file name is rendered as it is in a stderr line or a bell row only when it clears the bus's id grammar; any other has its line boundaries replaced and is shortened to the bell's width, so a name forges no second line. Clear all's answer is true: the chat drops only the chips of the ids the clear wrote, a press that cleared fewer cards than it asked is answered on the pressing page with the count and how many held messages stayed, and a press that cleared nothing is refused there in plain words. The footer offers Clear all only while a card on the board would take the clear, so a board of held messages alone loses the button, and so does a board of placeholders alone (provisional, awaiting, blocked and user-todo stand-ins), a wider change than the review named and a deliberate one; while a host's cards are unknown on a merged board the button stays offered on a board that has a card, since an absence proves nothing then and a press is answered truthfully; the view menu, the tag lens and the session box keep the card-count gate. The button's tooltip reads: clear the open cards (inbox-zero); Undo restores them. A held message stays until you approve or deny it. Upstream, the notices reader and its sweep: a notice row the parse skips is said on the bell as well as stderr, one row per file per episode naming the session and the keys, never a title or a value's text; every line the parse leaves out, whatever its shape (not JSON, not an object, no op, no key, an op that is not text), is said the same way before the sweep archives it as a skipped row. The writer's reader refuses a notice file it cannot read instead of answering nothing: a post over such a file assigns no revision, an expire retires nothing and says the fault rather than an absence, and the sweep leaves that session's rows live and moves on to the next session. The notice card's action door answers a read fault as a fault (nothing was done, try again) and not as the notice being gone, at both of its reads, so an action never runs twice. A fault shared by a directory's files is one bell row for the directory, with the count and the names that fit, and one stderr line per file; a fault one file has keeps its own row. A said-once episode ends on any successful read of its subject, wherever that read happens: a clean listing, a served memo entry, a store removed and recreated. Upstream, the bus's store readers: quarantine_get and quarantine_decide refuse a held record the bus cannot read or parse (a link with nothing behind it and a document that is null included) instead of answering that no such message is held, and the file is left for the kernel's reader. The bus never serves or gossips a held record under an id that is not its file's name or that it would refuse to decide, and a decide under such a name acts on nothing. A held record's body that is not text is served by GET /quarantine as its type name, with bodyType naming the substitution. An outbox or readbox record the bus cannot read is no longer moved aside with the sender's receipt closed as refused, as it had been since 2026-09-08: it is skipped, said once per file and fault as a bell row and a log line, left in place, and the receipt keeps reading pending; a store of two or more records none of which read is said once for the store with the count, and for the held-mail store that fault refuses quarantine_list, so GET /quarantine answers 503 for it. A recipient's inbox file the bus cannot read is still moved aside as before; that reader was not part of this change. A held-mail store a holding machine cannot list rides the peer exchange as one fault row naming the fault's kind and errno, never a path or a record's text, so the viewing machine's Held for approval elsewhere section names the holder's fault and counts it as no message instead of dropping the holder silently; the viewing kernel files no bell row for another machine's store fault (the holder's own kernel and bus ring the holder's bell, and the exchange payload cannot tell a clean listing from a pruned peer), and a viewing panel older than this build renders the fault row as one message held for approval until that machine updates.

The follow-on to that review, landed on the same branch before its re-review, changed the following. Fork-only, the kernel's held-mail reader and the clear door: a held record the kernel moved aside is said again by the error center each time the row carrying it has left the ring, after a kernel restart or once forty later rows have pushed it out, one row for however many asides stand (the count and the names that fit), until the file is renamed back without its .corrupt- suffix or deleted; the row names the file and what can be done with it, never its contents, and nothing is renamed to say it, so the fact that a record was moved aside outlives the ring. Clear all declines every re-listed placeholder id at the write and Undo passes over their rows at the read, both from one constant, _CLEARED_NO_LEDGER, which names the hold family beside the provisional, awaiting, blocked and user-todo stand-ins (the user-todo family had been missing from the no-session list too, and is a member now), so a press over stand-ins alone journals nothing and the first Undo after it restores the earlier deliberate clear, and a press that cleared cards beside stand-ins is answered on the pressing page naming the stand-ins for what they are, saying approve or deny only of held messages. Upstream, the bus's store readers: the hold summary a holder gossips carries a held record's from, to and origin as text (a container named by its type, a number spelled out) and its at as an integer or 0, so a type-wrong field on the holder reaches every peer's popover as a type name or an integer and never as an object the popover would format as its placeholder; the record stays listed and decidable. Upstream, the sheets: the two remaining rules that named the accent token beside the dark accent written out, the revealPulse glow in feed.css and the .staged-chip dashed edge in styles.css, read the accent through color-mix at their alphas, and the button-vocab census is rule-scoped over every rule of styles.css, feed.css and gear.css (a keyframe taken whole; a literal at alpha 0 or inside a var() fallback is no member), which widens this fix-tier PR by two rules that predate it. The reference's state-files paragraph lists quarantine/ among the postal stores and, since the bus's own reader of that store moves nothing, names the kernel's reader as the mover of a held record it read and could not parse or take, under the same naming, with no receipt changed since the relay was acknowledged when the hold landed, and states that the aside is named again until it is dealt with. The parity of the four readers is pinned: a row fault in a notice file, a notice file fault, a notices directory fault and a held record read and not parsed each file one refused row in one build and none on the next.
