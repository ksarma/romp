Upstream ships the same viewer, the same Comments panel and the same anchor map, so every item applies there once the slices before it have landed: a note whose markdown a browser nests inside an unclosed `<div align="center">` or `<details>` (a README's centred header, an Obsidian fold) becomes commentable after and inside the wrapper and the reader keeps their place through it; a comment on a code line or across two table cells saved from Raw paints in Rendered; a formula touched from Rendered hands the Raw view the formula itself; no refusal names a lexer token; overlapping comments read as one highlight and a click reaches every card under it; comments written by the CLI on a repeated passage take the copies in turn; a keyboard selection offers Comment; and unwrapping the marks of a long paragraph no longer costs the square of its links. Built as Slice 5 of plans/markdown-viewer.md on Slice 4's merge (fork PR #707, 2026-09-09-markdown-viewer-mdconfig), so the offer stacks on the four slices before it, each with its own entry (Slice 1 #390, Slice 2 #396, Slice 3 #407, Slice 4 #707). The files it changes are the ones those entries already name as fork-only candidates or as ported with them: anchor-map.ts and file-comments.ts travel with the file-comments candidate (2026-09-07-file-comments-tracked-changes) and its host tools, reader-place.ts with Slice 2, the sheets' `.fc-hl` region with the panel's CSS block, and the fixtures and tests with their modules; the guide and the plan port as they are. Nothing here reaches the kernel, the store format or the vendored CLIs: the stored quote stays the exact source slice (the paint-time reading of the plan's "strip cell delimiters" was the ruling of 2026-09-09), so the two host guards and the two e2e cases are regression guards on a contract upstream's host already keeps.
