The viewer fetched a gated figure while its placeholder said "Click to load". The review of fork PR 862 found it on main for an HTML img in WebKit (finding extra8-3, two refuters, a pre-existing defect); the review of this branch found it for an inline svg image in Firefox and in WebKit. mdBlock adopted the sanitized nodes into a live-document element before the figure chain ran. WebKit starts an img's fetch synchronously when the element's node document becomes one with a render tree; for the img figures, the servers' logs held no line in Chromium or Firefox before the chain ran. An inline svg's image is loaded by another path: at the base Firefox requested a gated one, spelt href or xlink:href, while its placeholder stood when the chain's work between the adoption and the gate was long (the second leg's 3000-paragraph note, both spellings, 3 of 3 runs; shorter notes in some runs or none; the counts are under "Run counts, the svg vectors" in the plan section), WebKit requested the xlink:href spelling in every run and the href spelling in none, and Chromium requested neither.
