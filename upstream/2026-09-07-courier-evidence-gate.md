The courier scanned every discovered session on every pass (a parse, a store load, the segment walk, the link-repair idempotence scan and the settle): 466 of 9696 samples in the 120 s steady profile, 31 plain store loads and about 0.17-0.21 s of CPU per idle pass on the 31-session bench, with nothing new in nearly all of them. The scan is now a gated stage like the planner and closer: it runs only when the pinned parse pair, the store trio or the episode log moved since the scan that last completed with no pending row and no backref; a scan that produced rows, reached another session's store through the link repair, stood down on a fallback store or raised leaves no stamp and runs again next pass. The settle is read at the five write sites from the store being written, once per written session, instead of for every session in the scan. A crash anywhere in the scan is caught per session as a pass-crash row and the pass goes on (before, a crash outside the parse aborted the tiers after the courier). The cross-host plant reads its candidate rows from one memoized parse of the ledger per version, shared with `_postal_row`, with the discovered-sender and horizon filters applied per pass. Bench (private copies of the state copy, judge paused, 31 sessions, 6 passes, two back-to-back pairs): courier idle 0.206/0.170 s and 31 loads per pass before, 0.005 s and 0 loads after (100% skipped); the whole triage pass loads 100 to 70 per pass. The ledger stays out of every signature: deliver() appends the sent row before the wake that produces the transcript atom, so the pinned pair re-arms the scan with the row in place; the gate inventory above `GATED_TIERS` names every input the scan reads.
