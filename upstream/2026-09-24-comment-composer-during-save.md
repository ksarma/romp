In the file viewer's Comments panel, a comment or a reply started while the previous comment was still saving showed the saving comment's words in its box, read-only under a Saving button that was not its own. The save's landing then closed the box just opened, and a refusal left the words in it, where Save filed them on the new target: a reply on another comment, or a comment on another passage. A Cancel during the save followed by a refusal lost the words with no row shown. The panel has one box for every composer; it is now emptied for a composer opened while it still holds the saving words, the saving look, loader and refusal row belong to the saving composer, a second composer's Save waits with the reason in its title and in a line with the romp loader under Save and Cancel, in place of the chord hint, and the settle acts on its own comment: a landing closes the saving composer only, and a refused comment comes back with its words and its own refusal row when no composer is open or the open one holds nothing of the person's, or, when it holds words typed, a rectangle drawn or a pending Re-place, waits in a note under it that names the comment and shows its words whole, with Bring it back (enabled once the composer holds nothing, its reason worded for that composer) and a dismiss; the comment comes back, once, when that composer closes. The viewer's close asks while a save is out, since a refusal after the close would lose the words, and its ask names every comment a yes would drop. Fix tier: the new test file is red over the base in each of its tests.
