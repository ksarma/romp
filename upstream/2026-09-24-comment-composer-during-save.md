In the file viewer's Comments panel, a comment or a reply started while the previous comment was still saving showed the saving comment's words in its box, read-only under a Saving button that was not its own. The save's landing then closed the box just opened, and a refusal left the words in it, where Save filed them on the new target: a reply on another comment, or a comment on another passage. A Cancel during the save followed by a refusal lost the words with no row shown. The panel has one box for every composer; it is now emptied for a composer opened while it still holds the saving words, the saving look, loader and refusal row belong to the saving composer, a second composer's Save waits with the reason in its title and in a line with the romp loader under Save and Cancel, in place of the chord hint, and the settle acts on its own comment: a landing closes the saving composer only, leaves the card being typed in where it stands and the keyboard where it was, and a refused comment comes back with its words and its own refusal row when no composer is open or the open one holds nothing of the person's, or, when it holds words typed, a rectangle drawn or a pending Re-place, waits in a note under it that names the comment and shows its words whole, with Bring it back (enabled once the composer holds nothing, its reason naming only buttons that composer shows) and a dismiss; the comment comes back, once, when that composer closes. Words the person typed are never written over: when a Re-place's drag has left them in the hidden box and a refusal arrives, they open as a comment on the file with the refused comment in a note under them (words typed for a reply become a comment on the file there). The viewer's close asks while a save is out and about words a Re-place hides, and its ask names every comment a yes would drop. A Re-place's Cancel, its Escape and the panel's close still empty words hidden in the box, as they do on the base. Fix tier: over the base the new stand-in test file is red in 64 of its 66 tests (the other two hold what the base already does) and the new browser leg in all four.
