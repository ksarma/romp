Two per-browser gear switches, both off by default. Share ON adds to the minute row, numbers and fixed-vocabulary identifiers only: nav, res, marks and env once per page (env again when the pane's own width/height aspect flips), vis, wsBytes and rafGap every row. Mute ON stops every clientDiag row from the browser on a kernel page (the collector's, the pane shim's, the reload core's and the shell's, the rows the shim and the shell queued for a redial included); in VS Code it stops the collector's rows alone, since the extension forwards a pane's breadcrumbs ungated. The collector flushes its minute on visibilitychange to hidden (iOS never fires pagehide on an app switch); the slowframe budget is the timer's minute and a hide flush does not re-arm it. The kernel's clientDiag handler admits an allowlist of top-level data keys per surface, cuts strings at 64 characters, refuses a page's row under the kernel's own surface, bounds what one row can have said on stderr, and stores a row over 24 KiB (derived from the collector's own caps, so no row it builds is touched) as a cap marker that keeps the pane (a perf minute row sheds its per-minute figures first, so the once-per-page fields survive); `romp perf client` skips the markers and counts both cap shapes. Motivated by the phone freeze rows of 2026-09-18; the offer waits on the user's word under the standing no-new-upstreaming rule.
