A click that only meant to focus the dashboard window landed on the banner's Update button (2026-09-10) and one click restarted every session on the box, cutting every turn in flight. Upstream serves the same banner from the same kernel code. The first click replaces the Update button with a label naming the restart (its counts, and whether other kernels restart too, read from the manager's registry) and shows a red Restart and a Cancel beside it; only Restart posts, so no second activation of one gesture (a double-click, a double-tap, a held Enter) lands on it, whatever click count the engine reports; the confirm also ignores a click count above 1 and a repeated keydown. The armed state is dropped by exact events only (Cancel, Escape through the shell's chain, a press outside the banner in the shell document, a press or a focus in a pane, focus leaving the banner, the window's blur, a hidden tab, every re-render of the banner), never a timer. The counts sum every backend the kernel runs (Claude and Codex sessions, each backend's restart_impact of one shape) and count as interrupted the sessions the restart gate's busy predicate names (a turn in flight or live background work); an answer without counts drops the held ones, so the label carries no counts while the SDK backend is still being built. Tier fix, not feature: the change corrects a defect that caused an incident (one focusing click restarted every session), the confirm step and the /update refusal are the minimal correction with tests that fail on the base, the hardened route is internal with one same-file client, and upstream tiered the same shape as fix (their #1027).
